# Sol Ultra fresh-context C3 structural-redesign adjudication

Decision: `REDESIGN_C3_ONLY`

## Verified evidence used

- V0.11 exact-O1 remained negative in pooled EE, so frozen-Q1 mismatch alone
  does not explain the failure.
- C1 owns focal opening bits and the complete current-slot network-energy
  difference.  OPS3-C2 owns focal persistence at `h=1..3`, including marginal
  projected network power.
- V0.11 active-beam steps were `1195` for `DROP_C3`, `1711` for AP,
  `1770` for M1-D, `1460` for exact-O1 drop, and `1941` for exact-O1 full.
- At exposed anchors, current-slot energy increased for the executed joint
  action in `28/30` AP, `22/22` M1-D, and `21/21` exact-O1-full cases.
- V0.9 PNFE was adverse: EE `-11.0636%`, bits `-5.3951%`, energy
  `+6.3736%`, and `0/3` positive lineages.  Its two known defects are not a
  credible explanation for that entire direction.

## Inference

The repeated active-beam and current-energy expansion is a strong mechanism
signal, although this development evidence is not causal proof or efficacy.
C3 should stop positively subsidising any unilateral action with nonzero
current marginal network-energy support.  It should not receive a second
numerical energy term because that would overlap C1/C2 ownership.

## Recommended ordered candidates

1. ZR: keep all current-slot per-victim non-focal losses; admit positive
   spatial redistribution only when the candidate has a bit-exact match to
   the C3-free reference in served vector, active beam/satellite sets,
   per-beam RF powers, and total network power.
2. HR: current-slot insertion-harm relief, centered at the C3-free reference;
   keep negative relief always and admit positive relief only under the same
   exact support indicator.

Both retain 28 native actions behind the one common safe mask.  Energy is a
Boolean teacher-support test, not an added target magnitude or deployment
mask.  If ZR passes it is selected regardless of HR magnitude; otherwise HR
may be selected only if it independently passes.  A pass authorizes only a
separate state-only Q3 learnability preregistration.

## Rejected next step

Do not test a full-horizon coalition residual next.  It would reopen the C1/C2
ownership seam, duplicate current/future terms unless carefully subtracted,
and require a new continuation-policy-dependent design.  It is not the
smallest justified response to the observed failure.
