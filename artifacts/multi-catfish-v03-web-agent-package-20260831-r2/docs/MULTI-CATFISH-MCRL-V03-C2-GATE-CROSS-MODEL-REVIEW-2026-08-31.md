## Inputs and common conclusion

Opus Max and a fresh-context Sol Ultra independently reviewed the bounded
canonical-fading C2 gate after the first one-trace engineering smoke. Both
accepted the formula-first temporal target and keyed matched-fork direction,
and both limited any passing claim to physical temporal headroom that may
authorize a learnability pilot. Neither review treated legacy `r2`, the old C2
certificate, or the positive smoke value as a decision input.

## Differences

- Opus judged `3 seeds x 3 anchors x 3 candidates` adequate for a weak
  existence screen if at least four positive traces span two seeds and two
  anchors.
- Sol judged three seed-worlds too clustered for the actual G-C2 gate and
  recommended five fresh seeds, service-safe receipts, per-seed coverage, and
  positive headroom in four of five seeds.
- Sol also identified that the current smoke recalibrated `lambda_0` from its
  evaluation seed, did not serialize served vectors, and covered only the
  incumbent-hold subroute.

## Root adjudication

The current authority adopts the more conservative five-seed design:

1. one TRAIN-only `lambda_0` from seed `2026082401`, frozen bitwise across all
   gate seeds and matching the C3 authority value;
2. five fresh seeds `2026083101...2026083105`, up to three anchors and three
   candidates per anchor, without early stopping;
3. exact reference/candidate served vectors and a downstream service guard;
4. `hold incumbent if legal`, otherwise the legal non-Main action with maximum
   predecision `candidate_sinr`, tied by physical ID and action ID;
5. positive means strictly `z2 > 0` on a service-safe complete trace; no
   magnitude threshold or mean-surplus test;
6. GO requires positive service-safe headroom in at least four seeds plus the
   coverage, censoring, error, random-field, and non-mutation gates sealed in
   `artifacts/c2-v03-gate-20260831/prereg.json`.

`3x3x3` is retained only as an optional engineering pre-gate. A G-C2 GO does
not establish observability, learnability, deployed argmax impact, superiority
to a neutral source, or held-out ratio-of-sums EE improvement.
