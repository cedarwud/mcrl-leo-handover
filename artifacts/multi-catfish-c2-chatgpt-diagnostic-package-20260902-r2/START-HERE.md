## Why this package exists

The project needs a genuine three-Catfish MCRL method with exactly
`C1 -> Q1`, `C2 -> Q2`, and `C3 -> Q3`. Deployment must remain one common safe
mask, direct unweighted `Q1 + Q2 + Q3`, one argmax, and one executed Main
action. Every Catfish must eventually have a positive marginal contribution to
final canonical network ratio-of-sums EE under `FULL versus DROP-Cj`.

C1 currently has strong positive evidence. Every C2 attempted so far is either
invalid, harmful, effectively zero, or unlearnable. C3 is positive only in the
old harmful-Q2 context and may become negative with a replacement Q2. The
purpose of this package is to let a fresh ChatGPT context diagnose the real C2
problem, audit the newly frozen OPS-3 formula, and recommend the fastest
scientifically valid resolution before any learner or long training.

Read `CURRENT-C2-DIAGNOSIS.md` immediately after this file. It is the compact
supersession-aware snapshot; older files remain included as evidence and may
contain stale lower-priority status paragraphs.

## Why C2 is hard

The focal action has a small, clean future physical path through carried
segment state, recurrence power, future link feasibility, and deterministic
orbital geometry. Larger network-total future differences also contain other
users' reactions through lagged load/interference observations. Those policy
cascades depend on the frozen Q1/Q3 lineage and were not predictable from the
old focal Q2 state.

An exact controlled downstream comparison can isolate segment state, but
future native action support can diverge between branches. A purely
decision-time orbital forecast avoids that support problem, but it becomes an
auxiliary shaping target rather than an exact realized total-effect identity.
The review must decide which scientific interpretation is defensible and most
likely to improve final EE.

## Required early interaction check

A C2 candidate is not acceptable merely because `FULL > DROP-C2`. Before any
long training, the same new Q2 context must also retain positive C1 and C3
marginals:

\[
\eta(Q_1+Q_2+Q_3)>\eta(Q_1+Q_3),
\]

\[
\eta(Q_1+Q_2+Q_3)>\eta(Q_1+Q_2),
\]

\[
\eta(Q_1+Q_2+Q_3)>\eta(Q_2+Q_3).
\]

Every pair need not beat every singleton. The three inequalities above are the
binding marginal directions; other pair contrasts are interaction diagnostics.
