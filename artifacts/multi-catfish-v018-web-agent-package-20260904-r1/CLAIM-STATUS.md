## Safe language for slides and figures

Use:

> “C1, C2, and C3 are complementary training-time views of one canonical EE
> objective; their learned contributions are combined only at one safe Main
> action.”

Use:

> “The V0.18 analytic diagnostic supports a relational-ZR learner attempt.”

Do not use:

> “C3 has been proven to improve EE,” “the full method wins,” “the three heads
> are synergistic,” or “the learned Q3 is validated.”
