# Claim ceiling and forbidden upgrades

## Permitted conclusions

- The copied method and gate contracts define a falsifiable LC-SRS
  observability question and a frozen raw informed-versus-placebo predicate.
- The supplied R6 values fail the frozen raw sign-gap requirement because
  `0.03776435045317217 < 0.05`.
- The supplied label prevalence and development-only balanced accuracies may
  motivate a prospective metric-design question; they do not change R6.
- The composition replay/runtime defect and the raw learner predicate are
  separate axes. A repair may establish execution integrity only.
- The current five-arm code is admission/receipt plumbing that requires an
  injected real adapter; its presence is not a physical five-arm result.
- A fresh TRAIN gate can be proposed only through a new pre-outcome contract
  that freezes estimands, raw and balanced reporting, worlds, seeds, formulas,
  physical/composition/service predicates, and a no-second-revision rule.
- Fable CSE/EC can be discussed as candidates or hypotheses. The root audit's
  counterexample prevents calling the summed unilateral CSE construction an
  exact simultaneous potential without a new proof.

## Forbidden conclusions

- Do not call R6 a scientific pass, a failed C3 mechanism, a deployment
  result, or EE efficacy solely from the fit numbers.
- Do not replace the failed raw sign predicate with balanced accuracy after
  seeing R6 outcomes, tune the sign threshold, select a favorable seed, or
  revise the gap threshold.
- Do not call development evidence held-out efficacy, physical superiority,
  a successful full-vs-ablation marginal, or a complete five-arm execution.
- Do not auto-promote CSE or EC, merge them into an unbounded candidate family,
  or use a post-outcome formula/rescaling/sign/horizon/seed change.
- Do not open TEST, run a simulator, train, launch episode-policy evaluation,
  or edit package/project files.
- Do not treat a corrected `_network_snapshot` import, replay digest, or
  receipt hash as a scientific outcome.
- Do not turn a source path, checksum, passing test, or running process into
  controller/human acceptance without the corresponding gate.

## Required separation

Every review conclusion should identify which of these it addresses:

1. scientific legitimacy of the estimand and evidence;
2. execution/integration readiness of the implementation seam;
3. process validity and preregistration discipline; or
4. a proposal for one exact next contract.

The final token must be one of the four tokens required by the prompt and must
not be followed by any explanation.
