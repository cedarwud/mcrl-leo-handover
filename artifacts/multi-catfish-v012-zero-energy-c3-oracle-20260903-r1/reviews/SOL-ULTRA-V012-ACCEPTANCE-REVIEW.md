# Fresh-context Sol Ultra review of V0.12 acceptance

Date: 2026-09-03 UTC  
Review mode: fresh context; supplied evidence only  
Decision: `FRESH_WORLD_CONFIRM_ZR_REVISED_ACCEPTANCE`

## Review conclusion

V0.12 must remain formally STOP because its preregistered
active-satellite-step hard guard failed. The reviewer found no evidence in the
supplied panel that the ZR formula itself is structurally defective relative to
canonical EE.

The satellite-step count is not scientifically necessary as an EE hard guard
when modeled total trajectory energy is measured exactly. It is justified only
if satellite activation represents a separately declared operational cost or
risk omitted from that energy measure.

## Recommended next gate

Run one prospectively frozen, paired ZR-versus-DROP confirmation on entirely
fresh TRAIN worlds. Keep the ZR teacher, masks, tie breaking, Q1 lineages,
10-step horizon, estimands, thresholds, and stopping rule fixed. Require:

- positive pooled and per-world canonical ratio-of-sums EE;
- at least two of three EE-positive lineages in every world;
- service noninferiority;
- exact accumulated trajectory-energy nonincrease, pooled and per world;
- all existing formula, mechanics, support, exposure, and current-slot joint
  support checks.

Keep beam-step and satellite-step counts as diagnostics, not hard stops. Retire
HR from the confirmation because it produced no distinct action or outcome.

Only after a passing confirmation should a separate state-only Q3 learnability
gate be preregistered on disjoint TRAIN worlds. A learnability pass would not by
itself establish learned-policy efficacy or held-out generalization.

## Evidence boundary

This review is a methodological adjudication of supplied TRAIN-world oracle
evidence. It is neither an independent simulator run nor an efficacy result.
