# Round 10B — Statistical estimand and inference review
You are the statistician a journal would consult. The attached files define the endpoint and inference for a simulation study comparing policies on energy efficiency.
Design: pooled ratio η = ΣB/ΣE over a panel; contrasts are relative gains η_FULL/η_DROP − 1; the decision rule is a 2.5th-percentile bootstrap lower bound above +0.5 % relative, jointly with QoS non-inferiority margins (availability difference above −0.5 pp; handover rate and Φ-priced cost relative change below +5 %), as an intersection–union test on one primary configuration. Resampling is a two-way pigeonhole bootstrap over TLE dates × learner seeds with arms paired inside each resample, recomputing ΣB/ΣE per draw. Measured calibration on synthetic receipts: two-sided interval coverage 0.86–0.92 against 0.95 nominal at 5–24 seeds; three-contrast conjunction power 0.38–0.68 at a planning alternative of +2 %.
1. Is the pooled ratio of sums the right estimand for "energy efficiency of a policy", or should it be a ratio of expectations, a per-world ratio, or a regression-adjusted contrast? What changes about the interpretation, and which do referees expect in simulation studies with unequal per-world energies?
2. The two-way pigeonhole bootstrap under-covers at these seed counts. What are the options (studentisation, BCa, subsampling, cluster-robust delta method, permutation for the null, exact/randomisation tests) and which is defensible here with 16 seeds and ~160 dates? Give the one you would require and the diagnostic that shows it works.
3. Intersection–union with three EE contrasts and three QoS margins: what exactly is controlled, what is not, and what happens to interpretation if regimes R1–R7 are also reported? What must be pre-registered to keep the primary claim clean?
4. Non-inferiority margins on availability, handover rate and a priced handover cost were chosen by the authors without an external anchor. How should such margins be justified in a simulation study, and what wording is defensible if they cannot be anchored?
5. Power: with the measured 0.68 conjunction power at +2 % and irreducible seed-level variance, what should the paper claim, and what design change gives the largest improvement per unit of compute?
