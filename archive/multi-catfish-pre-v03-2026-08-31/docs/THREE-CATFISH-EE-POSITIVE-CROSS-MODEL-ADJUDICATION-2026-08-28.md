# Three-Catfish EE-positive cross-model adjudication

Date: 2026-08-28  
Status: independent design review; not empirical evidence or training authority

## Question reviewed

Can Multi-Catfish MCRL defensibly use three independent training-time Catfish
roles, route each role one-to-one into Main `Q_1/Q_2/Q_3`, require every
singleton to improve fresh-seed Main-only system EE and its own canonical
objective, and require C123 to be best, without changing EE or `r_1/r_2/r_3`?

Reviewers were explicitly asked to search for simpler alternatives and failure
modes, not to approve the live proposal. Fable Max reviewed the physical causal
paths and minimum controls. Fresh-context Sol Ultra separately reviewed the
architecture and then adjudicated Fable's C2 objection. Local code evidence was
checked before controller synthesis.

## Shared findings

Both reviews agreed that:

1. the target is coherent as a preregistered empirical success condition, not
   as an algorithmic guarantee;
2. the current full-vector/all-head carrier must not proceed;
3. the next Main interface is exact canonical replay plus diagonal donor loss:
   `C1 -> Q_1^M`, `C2 -> Q_2^M`, `C3 -> Q_3^M`;
4. C1 may retain its private, sealed, offline Phase-I EXP/ACRM lineage under a
   unique and narrow exception; C2/C3 may not select realised outcomes;
5. C3's strict one-user load-gap identity is valid locally, while trained
   Main-only EE remains empirical;
6. pre-outcome useful-bits/EE forecasts do not guarantee stochastic outcomes;
7. every role needs a same-support random/simple control and must beat it on
   both its direct endpoint and EE; and
8. formal training remains NO-GO until the carrier, evaluator, consumer, and
   opportunity gates pass.

## Substantive C2 disagreement

Fable rejected generic persistence as an EE mechanism. In the current
simulator a handover has no explicit interruption time or energy cost, while a
long hold may increase recurrence power. Therefore

```text
fewer handovers -> higher EE
```

is not a valid causal statement.

Sol agreed with that objection but accepted a narrower Stage-0 candidate:

```text
avoid short-lived active beam/satellite and complete-system-energy churn
 -> reduce canonical r_2 events without a forecast EE loss
 -> Q_2-only donor experience
 -> empirical fresh-seed Main-only EE test
```

This is physically available because the simulator charges per-active-beam
circuit power, per-active-satellite baseband power, and PA supply power. It is
still conditional: recurrence and rate effects can reverse the sign, so the
full simulator power and a useful-bits/EE-surplus forecast proxy must screen
the Stage-0 support.

## Controller resolution

The user-set hard target is retained. C2 is redesigned as
**Activation-Churn-Aware Temporal Continuity**, not generic persistence:

- the frozen Main reference must leave the incumbent and cause a short-lived
  active-resource event or greater complete system energy;
- a candidate physical association remains valid and is held through a frozen
  horizon and first release;
- all non-focal physical actions remain identical in common-anchor forks;
- the candidate strictly improves system-total canonical `r_2` through release
  without delayed-event or service-loss surge;
- useful bits and the EE-surplus inequality are binary fading-off forecast
  proxies, never reward, ranking label, replay weight, or realised-outcome
  admission; and
- a minimum number of anchors must offer at least two certified choices, or no
  learned Q2 contribution has been identified beyond a stay heuristic.

Controls are frozen as Main reference, broader-support random persistence,
stay-if-valid, same-certified-support random, and learned C2 ranking.

The two other roles remain:

- **C1 Energy Frontier:** private sealed EXP/ACRM, canonical `r_1`, Q1-only
  routing, with zero, neutral, component, and direct-anchor controls.
- **C3 Spatial Load:** same-satellite high-to-low load relocation to an
  already-active destination, strict load identity, persistent full-power
  safeguard, useful-bits/EE-surplus forecast proxy, canonical `r_3`, Q3-only
  routing, and SAFE/LOAD/CERT/GAP/random controls.

## Hard outcome ruling

For each role, all four dimensions must pass:

1. direct canonical endpoint;
2. fresh-seed Main-only ratio-of-sums EE;
3. service/useful-bits/physics guards; and
4. distinct learned value beyond same-support simple/random controls.

If C2 or C3 improves `r_2/r_3` but fails EE, it is rejected as one of the three
EE-positive Catfish roles. It may remain only as a disclosed ablation. It may
not be counted, renamed, rescued by post-result dose/support changes, or removed
from a post-hoc "all surviving roles" treatment.

`C123` is always the fixed three-role treatment. It may be called best only if
all singleton gates first pass and it then beats baseline, all informed
singletons, all informed pairs, and dose/support-matched leave-one-role
references while preserving every direct objective and guard.

## Claim ceiling

This review supports only a coherent, falsifiable design and Stage-0
implementation direction. It does not show that any role improves EE, that C2
has sufficient support, that C3 is observable by Main, or that C123 is best.
Those questions require frozen, fresh-seed Main-only experiments.
