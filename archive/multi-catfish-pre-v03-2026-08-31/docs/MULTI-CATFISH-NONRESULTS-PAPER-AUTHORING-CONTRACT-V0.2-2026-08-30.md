## 3. Stable proposed method

### 3.1 One-sentence definition

Multi-Catfish MCRL uses three independent, training-only Catfish specialists
aligned with the three canonical rewards to generate three objective-aligned
classes of executed experience. A gated, diagonal routing operator applies
each specialist's real TD loss only to the matching Main objective DQN while
retaining the complete unmodified reward bundle for audit. Only Main selects
actions at evaluation and deployment.

### 3.2 Learner topology

- Main retains the original three objective networks.
- Catfish 1 owns one energy-efficiency specialist network.
- Catfish 2 owns one handover-continuity specialist network.
- Catfish 3 owns one load-balancing specialist network.
- The treatment therefore has four logical learner states and six online Q
  functions, plus their target copies.
- The three Catfish do not share a policy, replay memory, optimizer, RNG, or
  option state.
- A Catfish bundle retains the complete canonical reward vector, but its
  specialist-origin gradient reaches only the Main head bearing the same
  objective number. Non-target heads learn from canonical Main-origin replay.

### 3.3 Role contract

| Specialist | Canonical reward learned | Behavior support | EE relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 Energy-Frontier | unchanged `r1`, with ACRM confined to C1's own update | immutable `local_snr_greedy` source versus `masked_uniform`, C1-only prefill, plus online C1 exploration | direct first-objective path |
| C2 Policy-Aligned Forecast-Certified Temporal Fork | unchanged `r2` | fixed pre-outcome reference/candidate forks; both recompute the same frozen Main on their own branch state; only the candidate focal action is held for three offsets, followed by the complete candidate-local Main release; K0 fallback, K1 forced control, K>=2 `Q_2^F` ranking | support requires positive forecast EE surplus, positive hold/full system-R2 margins, no new non-focal outage, and branch-local Main alignment; realised Main-only EE remains empirical |
| C3 Spatial Load-Balancing, with separate power/service safeguard | unchanged `r3` | same-satellite relocation from a more loaded source to a less loaded, already-active destination, with a persistent load certificate and separate power/service safeguards | direct `r3` path; power and EE are supporting empirical endpoints only |

C3's power difference is part of the pre-outcome support/safeguard record and a
diagnostic. It is not a fourth reward, is never added to a load statistic, and
does not replace canonical `r3`.

### 3.4 Common Catfish operator

Every specialist applies the same four semantic steps:

1. **Trigger:** read only canonical action-time state, masks, physical
   identities, specialist-private option state, and its independent RNG.
2. **Propose:** select one legal specialist action or option without reading a
   realised reward, stochastic successor, future environment RNG, or oracle EE.
3. **Execute and retain:** execute the complete branch and retain its outcome,
   including adverse outcomes and early termination.
4. **Route experience:** update the specialist on its declared private view;
   after the Main-consumer gate passes, retain the complete atomic bundle and
   apply its real TD contribution only to matching `Q_j^M`.

### 3.5 Training and deployment phases

| Phase | Meaning in this paper | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|
| I | offline `local_snr_greedy` source and `masked_uniform` control construction, Source Gate A, and C1 replay prefill | not a dual rollout; not RIS DFT/WMMSE fidelity; prefill enters `D_1^F` only and never Main directly |
| II | independent Main/C1/C2/C3 collection, specialist updates, gated atomic experience routing, fixed-quota Main updates | experience is routed; actions and private rewards are not fused |
| III | evaluation and deployment using Main only | all Catfish doses are zero; no voting, auction, coordinator, intent exchange, or override |

### 4.3 C3 formula that must accompany its figure

For a same-satellite move of one focal user from source beam `v` to destination
beam `v'`, let the reference loads before that move be `U_{s,v}(h)` and
`U_{s,v'}(h)`. At each certified interval `h`, the two affected terms of the
P3 cost satisfy

```text
(U_{s,v}(h)-1)^2 + (U_{s,v'}(h)+1)^2
    < U_{s,v}(h)^2 + U_{s,v'}(h)^2
iff U_{s,v}(h) >= U_{s,v'}(h) + 2.
```

Equivalently, because
`sum_u r_{3,u}(h) = -sum_{s,v} U_{s,v}(h)^2`, the one-user
move changes the total canonical third reward by

```text
Delta sum_u r_{3,u}(h)
    = 2 (U_{s,v}(h) - U_{s,v'}(h) - 1).
```

The source load includes the focal user and the destination load excludes it;
both are reference-fork, pre-move eligible loads. The integer `+2` condition
must hold at every certified interval. The certificate separately records
persistent system-power relief and service/useful-bit safeguards. Let
`Delta P^N(h)` be reference-fork system total power minus candidate-fork system
total power; require it to be nonnegative at every certified interval and
strictly positive at least once. C3 still learns canonical `r3`;
`Delta P^N(h)` records only the power mechanism. The
within-window `r3` improvement is true by construction, so empirical readouts
must extend through the first post-release interval and the episode total.
