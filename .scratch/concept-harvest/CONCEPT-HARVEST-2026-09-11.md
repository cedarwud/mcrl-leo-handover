### 1.1 Differences that make an old verdict non-transferable (the brief's six, corrected, plus eight more)

1. **Environment.** SAY: `family_b` had a hard cap of k_cap = 3 beams per window satellite. Users on a 4th-ranked cell were "cap-bumped" to r1 = 0 (`DOCS/catfish-explainer-package/02-collapse-mechanism.md:42-67`). Here there is no cap (H-OBS) and service is ≈ 0.998 (H-SERVE).
2. **Metric.** Every old catfish-era number is `argmax_EE`, a **mean over users of per-user ratios with unserved users counted as 0** (parts/B §0, verified in `CV2/score_inj_rung1.py:135-140`). SAY: "EE = −674·cap_bump + 601, R² = 0.945 over 25 arms" (`FBD/CATFISH-VS-DQFD-DESIGN-2026-07-15.md` §9.1). Pooled EE was only a side diagnostic, and there it was **nearly policy-insensitive**: "random 5.85e8 ≈ DQN_scalar 5.96e8, within 2%" (`FBD/HANDOFF-coverage-free-ee2-catfish-2026-07-05.md`). Here pooled EE spans 53.1 M (random) to 112.5 M (a rule), a factor of 2.1. The only old line scored on a ratio of sums is the August Phase-I line (parts/D COND-FB-AUG).
3. **Learning rate. The brief's framing is right but needs dating.** SAY: "Flipping ONLY the learning rate (0.01 → 0.001)… doubles argmax EE and lifts worst-user coverage from 0.312 to 0.698" (`FBD/COLLAPSE-ROOT-CAUSE-IS-LR-2026-07-20.md:3-6`). Every June to mid-July MODQN-family arm ran at lr 0.01.
   - **But the late-July and August negatives ran at lr 1e-3:** SCFZA catfish −20.37, full DQfD −83.15, margin-OFF demo replay −42.25, the capacity penalty +89 to +135, and Phase-I B/N 0.73.
   - These are the strongest old negatives. They must be answered on causes other than lr.
4. **Wiring. The brief overgeneralises.** SAY: no old trainer distilled from a frozen copy of its own main (parts/E §1, grep).
   - The "distilled over rounds" wiring belongs only to the June P4, 6-arm and route-C lines: "All arms warm-start from a READ-ONLY copy of (P)", rounds R0–R3, with the main trained by **supervised CE imitation, not env-reward TD** (parts/A COND-P4-6ARM, citing `FBD/p4-coverage-multiteacher-catfish-prereg-2026-06-24-v5.md` §6 and v6 §3).
   - That wiring is exactly where CA-CPBR ran. Every other line trained the main from scratch.
5. **Physics. Old power grew with load: `P_b = min(0.25 + 0.35·√L, 10)`.** SAY: "the entire +54.9% comes from OPENING MORE BEAMS and BALANCING LOAD" (`CV2/ORACLE-AND-CATFISH-ABLATION-VERDICT-2026-07-13.md:56-64`). Handover energy was zero there as here.
   - Here per-beam power is a `max`, the PA takes ~94.8%, and lighting a beam costs ≥ 6.267 W.
   - **The sign of "spreading" flips between the two projects.** Every anti-homogenisation potential or penalty therefore needs its sign re-derived.
6. **Defects.** See §2. In short: defect (a) splits the old evidence into two lineages. (b) is absent from every old environment. (c) is present in almost every family_b trainer.
7. **Decoder.** Most old catfish and injection arms trained through, or were scored through, a coordinated auction decode. SAY: that decode "delivers ~420 EE on its own" and is "exactly invariant" to per-user Q offsets and global rescales (`CV2/DECODE-TRANSFER-FINDING-2026-07-12.md:44-52, 386-388`). Q-side improvements were structurally hard to see. Here deployment is pure per-user argmax.
8. **Temporal structure.** SAY (family_b): 10 steps of 1 s each; the serving window is chosen at reset and frozen; the episode sees "0 / 5000" window exits; and the persistence probe measured "ΔEE at t+1 and t+2 = 0.0000 exactly" (parts/F Part 4; `CV2/NULLSPACE-PROBE-RESULT-2026-07-21.md:19-27`). Here see H-DYN.
9. **Teacher type.** The old better-than-learner teachers (F-mean, G, ORACLE, planners) were joint, congestion-game solvers. SAY: their per-user action "is a function of information the per-user OBSERVATION does not carry… fits LABEL NOISE" (`CV2/CONVERGE-PROBE-RESULT-2026-07-17.md:32-44`). Here the better-than-learner sources read only the learner's own observation (H-DEMO).
10. **Checkpoint selection.** Old runs selected the best-eval checkpoint on the **raw** scalar, which r1 dominates (defect c). The selected checkpoints were early: median 30% of training, and the faithful catfish's was **episode 99 of 3000 on all three seeds** (`CV2/TRAINING-DIVERGES-FINDING-2026-07-12.md:18-32`). Here the scored artefact is a declared checkpoint.
11. **Reward-driven collapse.** SAY: legacy r3 was max−min over *active* beams only. "r3 ≈ FULLY offsets the EE penalty (129% discounted)" and rewarded collapse (`CV2/R3-DISCOUNTED-RESULT-2026-07-14.md:25-31`). Here r3 = −U_{b_u} per user. It is misaligned in a different direction: it rewards spreading (erratum 25).
12. **Outage handling.** SAY: re-entry after an outage is charged φ2 in both projects (parts/D D-35). The r2/r3 free ride on unserved users (defect b) exists only here.
13. **Only one old concept was scored on pooled EE with a healthy lr and no decoder: the August Phase-I line.** It still had k_cap = 3 and defect (a) (parts/D D-33/D-34; parts/E row 15-16).
14. **Pooled EE here moves with the gain-following decision** (erratum 22: "What matters is *which option each user takes* (gain), not how many beams end up open"). The old line's levers were coverage and cap_bump, which do not exist here.

## 4. Ranked shortlist: at most six concepts worth trying here

Ranking criterion: is the mechanism plausible **given this project's measured physics and objective**? Whether it "worked" over there is not a criterion. Every test below is placed *after* B0 (D-2 landed), because (b) is absent from every old environment and porting does not bring protection against it (§2).

### #1. Observation-only specialist demonstrations as margin-free replay (X6; RETRY)

- **Idea.** Keep a permanent, separate buffer of real transitions generated by the hysteresis rules `A m=9/12dB`, plus `MAX_NOMINAL_GAIN` as a coverage source. Mix it into each minibatch at a small declared ratio. There is no imitation loss; TD alone decides what to keep.
- **Intervention point.** Experience.
- **Why plausible here.** The old failures (MOFF −42.25, INJ-1 null) were attributed to a *joint* teacher, whose per-user action the per-user observation cannot carry. That cause is absent here, because every better-than-learner rule reads only the learner's own observation (H-DEMO).
  - `A m=12` beats the learner on the learner's own calibrated scalar (+1.22 vs +0.90), so plain TD has a reason to value its transitions. This is not "imitating something the objective disapproves of".
  - The JSRL probe already shows guide states are novel to the learner (H-COV). That is the coverage mechanism of Yang et al. Fig. 4 (consolidated §3c), measured locally.
- **Code.** Old:
  - `SRC/injection_rung1/trainer.py:234-289`: ρ-replace inside the batch budget.
  - `SRC/route_b_factorial/trainer.py:77-105`: the RNG decoupling that keeps ON/OFF lockstep; this was a review blocker in the old project.
  - `SRC/route_b_factorial/external_inject.py:76-82`: the frozen `.npz` pool format with sha pin; the margin-free form.

  Here: the rule implementations in `HERE/feasible-frontier/scripts/frontier.py`, and the 7-tuple schema in catfish-surface §6. No demonstration producer exists yet; catfish-surface estimates ~130–150 lines.
- **Cheapest test.**
  - Zero training, minutes:
    - (a) BC top-1 of `A m=12` from the 112-d observation, the declared "Representability" gate.
    - (b) Visitation divergence of `A m=12` rollouts against checkpoint rollouts on the frozen harness, using the `jsrl_blocks.py` instrument.
  - Then one 500-episode pilot: OFF vs DEMO vs NULL_GUIDE (a random-network demonstrator), greedy pooled EE, n = 24. About 800 s per arm on the server, the PENALTYARM rate.

### #2. Advantage-gated large-margin loss toward an observation-only specialist (P4 + X7 + P8; ADAPT)

- **Idea.** Apply DQfD's `J_E = max_a[Q_w(s,a) + m·1(a≠a_D)] − Q_w(s,a_D)` on the scalarised `Q_w`, with three changes:
  - the margin is `m = k·std(valid Q_w)`, re-measured on this project's calibrated scale;
  - it is applied only where the demonstrator's advantage `m_η(s, a_D, a_L)` > 0;
  - λ decays.
- **Intervention point.** Penalty (an extra loss term).
- **Why plausible here.**
  - Joint-teacher cause: absent (see #1).
  - "Spread-the-users over-correction" cause: points the other way here (H-SPREAD).
  - Margin-scale cause (1,884×): fixed by `qw_sd` scaling.
  - Hard-anchor risk named by DQFDGROUND: handled by the gate. Where the learner's own action already has the higher estimated `B − ηE` value, the term is silent.

  The gate is the old project's never-built "objective-based imitation" (`FBD/CATFISH-VS-DQFD-DESIGN-2026-07-15.md:281-300`), stated in the difference form the consolidated ruling grounds. **Using `m_η` to gate imitation is new and must be labelled ours** (consolidated §1).
- **Code.**
  - `SRC/injection_rung1/margin.py`: 68 lines, torch only (`dqfd_margin_loss` 49-68, `qw_valid_spread` 32-46).
  - `scale_mode: qw_sd` at `SRC/injection_rung1/trainer.py:196-208`.
  - Here, `update()` needs one combined backward across the three heads (catfish-surface §8, ~20 lines).
- **Cheapest test.** Zero training, on the frozen checkpoint:
  - (a) the std of calibrated `Q_w` over valid actions, which sets the margin scale;
  - (b) on `A m=12`-visited states, the fraction where the learner's greedy action already equals the rule's. If it is near 1, the gate never fires and the mechanism is inert.

  Then add it as one arm next to #1 (DEMO + gated margin vs DEMO), so the margin's increment over pure replay is what gets measured.

### #3. CA-CPBR, adapted: catfish-only potential shaping, per-user potential, gap-keyed η, containment (R3 + R4; ADAPT)

- **Idea.** Shape the catfish's reward only, with `F = γΦ(s',t') − Φ(s,t)` and `Φ = η_t · Ψ(o_u)`.
  - Ψ is computed from the user's own observation and tied to the numerator lever: the incumbent's normalised log nominal gain, or −log(best/incumbent gain), which is exactly the hysteresis statistic.
  - `η_t` is set per round from the **live main's pooled-EE gap to the best rule on a frozen probe set**, frozen within a round.
  - Transitions reaching the main carry the unshaped reward.
- **Intervention point.** Reward. It reaches the main only through experience.
- **Why plausible here.** CA-CPBR was never exercised. It was inert because the main was a planner-distilled student whose collapse level stayed constant over 4 rounds (difference #4). Here the main trains from scratch, and the gap signal moves across checkpoints.

  The two components tied to the old environment are replaced:
  - the assignment-entropy Ψ rewards spreading, which is EE-negative here (H-SPREAD);
  - the collapse key has no working instrument here (H-COLL).

  **The replacement for the collapse-adaptive coefficient is the owner's suggestion: key the same control loop on a signal that varies here. I judge it legitimate:**
  - Dynamic PBRS telescopes within each round, so an η that is constant per round and set from a measured gap keeps the per-round invariance argument.
  - Containment makes the Ng-1999 objection moot for the deployed agent.
  - The stale-η problem in replay is the same one as `B − ηE`'s, with the same fix: recompute at sample time from the stored raw state.

  Its own honest limit stays: it preserves the catfish's optimum, so it only steers transiently.
- **Code.** `ARCH/demo_guided_catfish/ca_cpbr.py`: `phi_k` 276, `shaping_F` 312, `CollapseSchedule` 204, 400 lines, 11 unit tests including B1 invariance on tabular VI with a non-vacuous negative control. Controls in `ARCH/demo_guided_catfish/arms.py:45, 136`: dose-matched static η and an anti-correlated schedule. The CF net there inherits defect (a) (parts/E row A5); route its critic through the shared-a′ update.
- **Cheapest test.** Zero training, minutes:
  - (a) The earned-validity gate (I6): Spearman between `F` and the transition's realised step contribution to `bits − η·joules`, on harness rollouts of the checkpoint and the rules. The old Ψ_r2 scored +0.02 and was decorative; a comparable result here kills this row.
  - (b) Confirm the η signal moves: the OFF arm's pooled-EE gap to `A m=12` across its episode-100 to 500 checkpoints (`HERE/penalty-arm/results/eval-traj-*`).

### #4. Satellite-exit hazard, as a state feature and as a replay/injection key (Rep2 + X14; RETRY)

- **Idea.** Give each user the incumbent's residual visibility τ_exit and elevation rate. Use the same quantity to key which transitions are replayed or injected, for example forced-handover-imminent states.
- **Intervention point.** Representation (state), plus experience (routing key).
- **Why plausible here.** The old kill was a geometry fact of family_b: a frozen window, 10 × 1 s episodes, 0/5000 in-episode exits, and the κ_HO key 99.6% zero (§3 X14). Here satellites set *within* episodes (H-DYN).
  - The frontier is carried entirely by hold/switch rules that know nothing about exits.
  - [I] A policy that holds on gain *and* switches just before an exit has information no rule on the frontier uses. That is a concrete way for a learner to occupy ground the rules cannot reach, which is exactly what FEASFRONT says the learner currently lacks.
- **Code.** `FABLE/r3fixed/sibling/{hazard_features.py, criticality_features.py, criticality_stratifier.py, test_criticality_features.py}`. These were built for family_b; the features must be recomputed from this project's ephemeris (`src/mcrl/env/ephemeris.py`, `dwell.py`).
- **Cheapest test.** Zero training:
  - (a) the degeneracy pre-flight that killed it before: per-user variance and zero-fraction of τ_exit over the harness;
  - (b) a rule screen: `A m=12` plus "switch to best if the incumbent exits within k steps", against `A m=12`, n = 24, fresh env per cell. If exit information cannot improve even a rule, drop the row.

### #5. Asymmetric discount (M2) and multi-step competitive credit, gated on measured persistence (R6 + R2; RETRY)

- **Idea.** The catfish values the future more (γ_CF 0.99 against the main's 0.9). Its competitive credit is computed over W steps.
- **Intervention point.** Reward (the horizon over which a transition's value is counted).
- **Why plausible here.** The old verdict "structurally inert" was caused by zero step-to-step persistence ("ΔEE at t+1 and t+2 = 0.0000 exactly"). The source thesis ranks this component as its *largest* effect, attributing it to cross-step structure family_b lacked. Here state carries over through the incumbent, the lagged loads and segment-anchored power (H-DYN).
- **Code.** `SRC/catfish_faithful_familyb/` (γ_CF, the `minus-discount` preset). parts/E calls it the most portable catfish because it uses item replay and the same 7-tuple, but it must route through the shared-a′ `update()`.
- **Cheapest test.** Zero training: the I2 persistence probe on this harness. From checkpoint states, flip one user between hold and best at step t, roll the same policy forward, and measure Δ pooled bits and joules at t+1 and t+2. Non-zero persistence licenses one γ_CF arm; zero closes the row.

### #6. Endpoint-gap-keyed injection thermostat (X4 + X3 + X15; ADAPT)

- **Idea.** Replace the fixed 70/30, random-period intervention. The injection ratio, or the JSRL guide horizon, is set in proportion to the live main's clipped pooled-EE gap to the best specialist on a frozen probe set. It is drawn from whichever specialist leads on that probe.
- **Intervention point.** Experience (the schedule).
- **Why plausible here.** The old project repeatedly recorded early-transient help followed by late harm:
  - the conduit help window, +155 at 1500–2000 episodes, decaying to −423;
  - the Phase-I peak at episode 1400;
  - ACRM credited only at episodes 1000–1200.

  An injection that switches itself off as the gap closes is the direct response, and literature ties injection to sample efficiency, not asymptote. The old trigger signals do not separate trained from random here (erratum 24), so the key must be the measured gap.
- **Code.** Not built in the old project (08-21 surviving direction; 07-14 "S2 injection thermostat"). The ρ knob is in `SRC/injection_rung1`; the annealing schedule is in `CV2/PACK-MINI-SDD-2026-07-19.md:34-97`.
- **Cheapest test.** It needs #1 running first. Then ON with fixed ρ against ON with the thermostat, at the same gradient budget. The gap probe costs ~24 evaluation episodes per measurement.

**Already scheduled, and the harvest agrees, with one change:** bounded and contained ACRM (R1; erratum 26's four arms). The change: compute `r^CF − r^M` on the endpoint-aligned step `B − ηE`, not on r1. Otherwise the competition rewards the catfish for winning on a quantity that is anti-aligned with the endpoint.
