## 1. Selection-time scoring uses a declared fading margin (remedy for the nominal-vs-realised gap)
Selection scores are computed on margin-adjusted channel gains: for every link, ĥ_margin = ĥ_nominal · q₁₀, where q₁₀ is the **10th-percentile quantile of the declared keyed fading distribution** (shadow + scintillation) at that link's actual elevation, evaluated from the sealed channel model in closed form (no new telemetry, no realised draw). The score is internally consistent: F_m(a) = B_m(a) − η_ref·E_m(a) − Φ(a) with service, ACM mode, required power, bits and energy all recomputed under ĥ_margin. Rationale for the 10 % level: the standard 90 %-availability link-budget convention; it is not fitted to any outcome. Diagnostic only (never decision-bearing): the same anchors rescored at q₀₅ and q₂₅ with the top-1 agreement rate. KAT: with the fading distribution collapsed to a point mass, margin scoring is bit-identical to nominal scoring. **Executed physics, labels, the committed profile's evaluation and the endpoint remain unchanged at 48 boundaries with realised fading.**

## 2. C2 enters the set-level selector as a tie-break only
The set-level continuation term ranks only among the maximisers of the immediate margin-adjusted score (deterministic tie-break; ties defined by a declared 1e-9 relative tolerance). Q2's separate role in forming the per-user proposal a⁰ is unchanged. Before the formal matrix, the engine reports the three-way diagnostic that separates an implementation defect from forecast error from a genuine trade-off: (a) at matched anchors, ΔI_N ≤ 0 and ΔI_N + Σ_h Δc_h^N ≥ 0 for the immediate-optimal vs continuation-optimal profiles; (b) per offset, the split of (ΔF_realised,48 − ΔF_nominal,snapshot) into an integration error and a fading error; (c) the **joint future continuation minus the sum of singleton future continuations** (the round-8 suspect: immediate C3 corrects only the current interaction, so summed per-move continuations can stay optimistic at every future offset). The diagnostic is reported, not gated.

## 3. Coordination is measured by net collision avoidance, and Ψ is additionally re-anchored
At matched anchors, with D(a) = Σᵢ dᵢ(a), a_D = the additive-selection profile and a_C = the interaction-aware profile (identical anchors, catalogue, margin and C2 rule): report V_CA = [F(a_C) − F(a_D)]/κ together with both components (interaction loss avoided, singleton value sacrificed), unclipped, plus the frequency of reversals where D(a_D) > 0 and D(a_D) + Ψ(a_D) < 0. Additionally, the decomposition is reported **twice**: anchored at the carrier a⁰ (as now) and **re-anchored at the certified S_UNI local optimum u**, where dᵢ^u ≤ 0 by construction, so any improving joint escape must have Ψ_A^u > 0. The slice's negative Σg_I measures interaction loss *incurred* around the carrier and is not evidence against coordination. The operational certificate remains realised pooled EE against certified S_UNI; V_CA is a mechanism certificate.

## 4. Admission certificates: load-bearing vs informative, and a pre-declared trichotomy
Load-bearing for the a-r0 admission decision: (i) S0's **realised** pooled-EE gain reported beside its nominal prediction; (ii) S0 vs **certified** S_UNI (matched information, objective, budget and fallback accounting; termination certificate present, else the comparator is labelled budget-limited and the contrast is not load-bearing); (iii) the corrected factor-oracle marginals under the final C2 rule; (iv) realised availability/QoS, physics validity and deadline behaviour with timeout/fallback outcomes counted in B and E. Informative but not decisive: J1 − U_all (two purely additive good moves can exceed the best single move). Diagnostics only: cap-hit, plateau share, ACM distribution, lit beams.
**Pre-declared outcome trichotomy (a-r0 only):**
- `ADMIT_FULL` — all load-bearing certificates pass, including S0 > certified S_UNI by more than the certified numerical error, and S0's realised gain over the carrier BASE ≥ +1 %.
- `ADMIT_C1C2` — S0's realised gain over BASE ≥ +1 %, C1 and C2 factor-oracle marginals positive, QoS/validity/deadline pass, but S0 > S_UNI is not established: training is admitted with C3 carried as an evaluated layer; the learned Level-B test (FULL vs DROP_C3) still runs and may still succeed, since a learned coordinator and an oracle removal are different estimands.
- `NOT_ADMITTED` — otherwise: contingency ladder rung 1 (regimes R1–R7 in the sealed order).

## 5. Objective-gap note (documentation, no change)
η_ref is frozen at calibration, so a positive surplus B − η_ref·E does not by itself imply a higher realised pooled ratio than an arbitrary comparator (Dinkelbach updates the multiplier; this design does not). Every claim and certificate is therefore stated on realised pooled ΣB/ΣE, as already sealed. The paper's method section states this once.
