**The missing lever is uncertainty-aware configuration scoring:** replace point nominal scores with a prospectively fixed conditional expected/robust objective using deployment-available channel statistics and independent synthetic scenarios. Keep its catalog and other rules fixed.

The direct motivation is EXACT_ZR’s +0.985%, weaker NOMINAL_ZR support, and EXPECTED_ZR’s stop (**06**, §1, rows 14–15; **09**, item 6). That history demands testing sensitivity to unavailable channel information; it does **not** predict that uncertainty-aware scoring will succeed. S0’s mean rank correlation of 0.91 does not establish reliable signs for small selected gains (**04**, “Information-gap diagnostic”).

2. **None of the declared values is indefensible as a bounded engineering probe; none is established as physically optimal.**

* **Margin 0.1%:** Dimensionally valid because both margin and surplus are bits. Describe it as a fixed small intervention buffer, not a confidence bound or 0.1% EE guarantee. Do not derive it from R7’s −0.049%: that is a different estimand.
* **Cadence 3:** Evaluation every **90.24 seconds** is a transparent threefold frequency reduction. It is not a measured LEO coherence or dwell time.
* **Lock 3:** State precisely: the next three decision epochs forbid coordinator deviations; release occurs at \(t+4\), **120.32 seconds** after the triggering decision. BASE remains free to move the user.
* **Persistence \(H\le3\):** Inheriting OPS-3’s three offsets provides an auditable rationale; terminal truncation avoids inventing post-episode consequences. The inherited \(\kappa\) is dimensionally compatible, but its use as a persistence-risk price lacks calibration.
* **One-step lookahead:** Adding one **30.08-second** interval is the smallest extension that exposes delayed consequences. State the frozen-position/dwell assumptions and absence of a longer terminal value.

These justifications describe deliberate scope and computational limits, without implying optimization (**01**, variant rows and projection sections).

3. **Keep lowest mean total latency as the planning-priority rule.**

“Largest EE margin” explicitly selects the most favorable noisy outcome among eight contrasts. “Fewest interventions” is a poor deployment proxy: one evacuation can move many users, while BASE fallback can still trigger handovers.

Freeze comparable hardware, threading, timing boundaries, cache treatment and scheduling. Report p95, maximum and deadline misses: V-C’s low average can conceal slow active decisions. Mean latency establishes average computational cost, not real-time feasibility.

Latency selection **does not remove multiplicity**: eligibility already depends on observed EE. A barely positive SUPPORT result remains development evidence. Preserve the sealed distinction between latency reporting and the kill gate (**01**, “唯一 kill rule 與 progression,” “完整報告與成本”; **03**, §§5–6).

5. **If every tested coordinator fails, run one fixed same-state shadow replay on lite’s archived trajectory.**

At each saved predecision state, evaluate the committed lite configuration and that state’s frozen BASE proposal under identical realised fading keys; discard the counterfactual branch. This needs approximately one additional physical evaluation per decision, with no new policy search.

Using the completed panel’s pooled \(\eta_{\rm BASE}\) **only offline**, decompose total \(B-\eta_{\rm BASE}E\) into:

* Immediate intervention benefit relative to BASE **at the same lite state**.
* The remainder attributable to evaluating BASE on lite’s visited states rather than BASE’s own trajectory.

Separately compare nominal and realised paired gains at the original \(\eta_{\rm ref}\), keeping price mismatch distinct from model error. Positive immediate benefit overwhelmed by the remainder points toward downstream state consequences; negative immediate benefit shows that S0’s local advantage did not transfer. This is an accounting decomposition, not proof that a particular tracking mechanism caused failure (**03**, §4; **04**, “Information-gap diagnostic”).
