     7	
     8	## Two things it closed for free
     9	
    10	**The off-axis defect is NOT shared.** The sibling's 2026-06-04 audit found its base channel
    11	lacked off-axis, which **confounded** its `NO_A4_ADVANTAGE` verdict and its
    12	"collapse is reward-optimal" framing. **V0.25's related sealed-v1 observation defect was
    13	repaired in Q1 v2.** My lead was worth checking and it came back negative. Closed.
    14	
    15	**Four objective variants are on record as tried and failed** over there, and the rejection
    16	lines are the durable output: `kappa`-allocated per-user EE (**the ADR derives a reverse
    17	incentive**), angle-aware EE as a reward variant, proportional-fair angle-aware EE, and
    18	handover-aware EE. **Do not re-run these.**
    19	
    20	## 1. ADOPT the direction — an additive contribution that closes exactly to pooled EE
    21	
    22	Give every per-user term an additive contribution whose **sum is the instantaneous system EE**,
    23	and report ratio-of-sums across time rather than a mean of instantaneous ratios.
    24	
    25	**Why this is the top item.** It attacks the defect `BASIN2` measured directly: **the objective
    26	does not track the metric** — first-improvement from `RSS_MAX` descends from `41.621560` to
    27	`31.812902`, and the six-arm `F` order disagrees with the EE order. This project's decision
    28	score is `sum_u delta(u, a(u)) + psi`. **If each `delta` closed to system EE, the sum would be
    29	the metric rather than a proxy for it.**
    30	
    31	**Gated on `ETAFIX`** (dispatched 18:40Z), which asks whether any `eta` makes the `F` order
    32	match the EE order. **If some `eta` does, the defect is an exchange rate and this is not needed.
    33	If none does, the defect is structural and this is the repair.** Those are mutually exclusive
    34	and one job settles it.
    35	
    36	**Not adopted blind:** the sibling's own status is "demonstrated to work **for algebraic and
    37	runtime closure**; effectiveness was not established", and its ADR-003 is archived. Closure is
    38	not efficacy.
    39	
    40	## 2. ADOPT the cheap gate, not the bundle — supervised ranking before DQfD
    41	
    42	The proposal is DQfD-style demonstrations from `RSS_MAX` with a large-margin ranking loss.
    43	The demonstrator is project-local and already measured: **41.621560 Mbit/J at full 1200/1200
    44	service, using strictly less information than the learned selector**, differing from `a0` on a
    45	median **92.5/100** users with **every** differing choice at lower nominal gain.
    46	
    47	**The report's own sequencing is right and I adopt it**: because the sealed head loses to plain
    48	linear on level calibration in all three routes, **Q1 v3 plus a linear supervised ranking gate
    49	is the cheaper first test**; the replay/pretrain/L2 bundle is justified only if that gate fails.
    50	
    51	**Do not treat the sibling's DQfD failure as a veto.** It was measured on an obsolete load-only
    52	contract that its own later record rules out for current-physics claims — ON `440.63` vs OFF
    53	`523.78`, worse in 6/6 seeds, then invalidated. **A caution, not a verdict.**
    54	
    55	**Q1 v3 stays regardless.** DQfD does not replace the feature; a demonstration loss cannot teach
    56	a head to rank on a quantity it cannot see.
    57	
    58	## 3. HOLD — joint-profile construction, deterministic before learned
    59	
    60	Addresses the single-scalar interaction bottleneck and the 10.78% a jointly constructed profile
    61	already beats the best per-user rule by. The sibling's status: **"cheap zero-learning
    62	optimisation closed 64-82% of the gap"** — as an executor, not a learned win.
    63	
    64	**Gated on `COORDVALUE`, `CEILING2` and `BEAMCOUNT`.** If coordination has no value at a good
    65	operating point, or the ceiling saturates at `|A| <= 2`, this is answering the wrong question.
    66	
    67	**And it changes the deployment boundary**, so it is disallowed outright if pure per-user
    68	argmax is a project requirement. **That is an owner question, not mine**, and it must be
    69	answered before any work starts, not after.
    70	
    71	## 4. ADOPT the direction — per-option congestion context, replacing the single scalar
    72	
    73	The interaction route currently reaches the decision as **one scalar** added to a sum over ~100
    74	per-user terms. **A scalar added once cannot represent which candidate option caused which
    75	occupancy-dependent consequence.** Option-indexed context can.
    76	
    77	**The sibling's `chi` recovered only 0.4% of its coordination gain** — but it was tested there
    78	as a de-collapse device, and this project **does not have that pathology**: `QCOLLINEAR` places
    79	it on the repaired side of the scale, `+0.087`-`+0.208` above a measured `0.2735` independence
    80	floor against the sibling's `+0.843`. **The structural argument transfers; the numerical result
    81	does not.**
    82	
    83	**Do not import the sibling's cap-specific "competitor rank" fields.** Derive only what this
    84	project's own physics supports: `required_se = rate_target * occupancy / bandwidth` and the
    85	1.65 W cap. Same boundary as Q1 v3's gain scalar, so the two compose.
    86	
    87	## What this ruling does not do
    88	
    89	**None of the four is authorised to run yet.** Three are gated on jobs already in flight, and
    90	one is gated on an owner decision about the deployment boundary. Each creates a **successor**
    91	arm; the sealed five-arm inventory is not mutated.
    92	
    93	**And a caution the sweep earns**: 40 of 44 concepts were rejected, most because they address a
    94	binding this project does not have. **That rejection ledger is the durable deliverable** — it
    95	exists so this sweep is not run again.

    59	## D. Standing obligations the controller keeps re-discovering
    60	
    61	These are recorded because each was already written down and then not applied.
    62	
    63	1. **Demand-capped is the field convention.** The literature credits `min(capacity, demand)`;
    64	   crediting surplus above contract is non-standard and flatters us. Recorded 2026-09-10,
    65	   unused all day until 09:05.
    66	2. **Reporting only pooled is against convention.** Papers that care about heterogeneity report
    67	   the **summed-per-user** form alongside and criticise pooled for hiding per-link allocation.
    68	   **Every number reported today is pooled-only.** From now on, summed-per-user is a required
    69	   column in every measurement prompt, and is to be backfilled on any rerun.
    70	3. **The owed-repair register** in `V025-CONTROLLER-FINDING-CORPUS-IS-SURROGATE-2026-09-10.md`
    71	   §5 is read before any work is proposed, not when prompted.

    73	   violated by `MARGIN_Q`, and `C2WIRING` exists because it was not applied to C2 either.
    74	5. **Both contradiction numbers travel together** — `+5.62%` and `-60%` — until `SIGNFORK`.
    75	6. **Every exact-term number is a ceiling**, never an achievable value, and is labelled so.
    76	7. **Worker prompts constrain peak RSS, not only process count.** Cost 57.4 GB and a near-OOM.
