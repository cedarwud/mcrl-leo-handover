=================== V025-CONTROLLER-TERMINOLOGY-2026-09-10.md
     1	# Controller note — what to call the thing the third route predicts, and why "coordination" was the wrong word
     2	Recorded 2026-09-10 after the owner said they had understood the design as three independent components and asked why coordination kept appearing. The confusion was mine to fix: I used one word for three different things all day. `DIAGNOSTIC_NOT_CLAIM`.
     3	
     4	## The structural fact the owner needed
     5	The three routes are **not three parallel, same-shaped things**:
     6	
     7	- the first and second are **per-user** — they score each user's candidate action on its own, and the reference proposal is a per-user argmax over their sum;
     8	- the third predicts **Ψ = ΔF(A) − Σ dᵢ**, which is *defined* as what is left over after the per-user terms are summed.
     9	
    10	**If users were independent, Ψ would be identically zero and the third route would have nothing to predict.** So the interaction is not an extra component bolted on — it is that route's entire job description.
    11	
    12	## The three things I called "coordination"
    13	1. the third route itself, which predicts the interaction residual;
    14	2. joint multi-user moves against one-at-a-time moves, which is a property of the **search**, not of any component;
    15	3. the ranking rule that combines the three heads' outputs.
    16	
    17	These are different objects. Using one word for all three is why the owner could not tell whether coordination was part of the design or something I had introduced.
    18	
    19	## Better names, by layer
    20	**The estimand — what the third route predicts.** *Interaction term* is the general-purpose name and fits our eight arms exactly, since they are the complete 2³ factorial. In cooperative game theory the same object is the **Harsanyi dividend**, the Möbius transform coefficient, and for coalitions of size three or more our Ψ is the sum of all Möbius coefficients above first order. Information theory calls the positive case **synergy**. Economics and networking call the cross-user part an **externality** — which our own target-design review already used, as *cross-user externality*.
    21	
    22	**The mechanism — why interaction exists here.** **Inter-user coupling**, and it has two concrete sources in this system: equal-airtime sharing inside a beam, where one more occupant raises the required spectral efficiency for *everyone* in that beam; and co-channel interference between beams through the cross-gain matrix. The solver is literally a **coupled power fixed point**. This name is better than "coordination" because it names the cause rather than the response.
    23	
    24	**The comparison — joint against unilateral.** The unilateral certified endpoint is a **Nash equilibrium** of the association game: no single user wishes to deviate alone. The bounded joint optimum is the coordinated optimum over that candidate family. The gap between them is an instance of the **price of anarchy**.
    25	
    26	That framing also explains the magnitude we measured rather than excusing it. Gaps of a few per cent between a converged equilibrium and the coordinated optimum are **typical** for association and load-balancing games. Our corrected span of roughly one per cent is what that literature would predict, and the load-regime sweep's failure to enlarge it — even pushed to two hundred users and a rate target that reaches 3.36 against a table maximum of 3.71 — is consistent with it being a property of the problem class rather than of our operating point.
    27	
    28	**The architecture.** A **permutation-invariant set head**, in the Deep Sets and Set Transformer line, which is the literature the decomposition-conditioning round already cited.
    29	
    30	## Recommended usage
    31	| what is meant | say |
    32	|---|---|
    33	| the quantity the third route predicts | **interaction term**, noting it generalises the Harsanyi dividend |
    34	| why interaction exists | **inter-user coupling** — shared airtime within a beam, co-channel interference between beams |
    35	| the joint-versus-unilateral gap | the efficiency gap between the unilateral (Nash) fixed point and the coordinated optimum, an instance of the price of anarchy |
    36	| the third head's architecture | permutation-invariant set head |
    37	
    38	"Coordination" is not wrong, but it is ambiguous across all four rows, and I used it that way for a full day.
=================== V025-CONTROLLER-OBSERVATION-C1-IS-SEARCH-GUIDANCE-2026-09-10.md
     1	# Controller observation — the first route already predicts what the 451 % search needs at every step
     2	Recorded 2026-09-10, arising from the owner asking why learning cannot approach the improvement a classical search achieves. This is **reasoning from measured facts, not a measurement**, and it is recorded as a candidate direction rather than a proposal. `DIAGNOSTIC_NOT_CLAIM`.
     3	
     4	## The structural observation
     5	The first route's target is `d_i = F(a_i, a⁰_{-i}) − F(a⁰)` — the surplus from user *i* deviating alone from the reference.
     6	
     7	The iterated best-response search that produces the entire improvement over the geometric baseline asks, at **every step**, which single-user move helps most. That is the argmax of exactly that quantity, recomputed at the current configuration.
     8	
     9	**The search that delivers the improvement computes this route's label at every one of its steps.** It makes about seventy-eight committed moves over four and a half passes, scanning every legal alternative each time — roughly twelve thousand evaluations per anchor — which is precisely why it takes about sixty-five seconds.
    10	
    11	So the first route is not ill-suited to its target. **It is attached to the wrong consumer**: its label is native to guiding a search, and it is currently used to rank perturbations of that search's *output*.
    12	
    13	## Where the improvement actually comes from
    14	| arm | pooled efficiency, corrected rule |
    15	|---|---:|
    16	| geometry-only baseline | 7.93 |
    17	| **iterated unilateral, not learned** | **43.71** |
    18	| bounded joint search, exact evaluation | 44.27 |
    19	
    20	The learned layer sits downstream of the second row. It **inherits** that improvement and was never asked to produce it; its entire working range is the third row's margin over the second, about one per cent. That ceiling is an architectural consequence, not a property of the problem — the problem has the larger margin, and it was assigned to a component that does not learn.
    21	
    22	## Whether the three-route design would survive a reattachment
    23	The roles map naturally onto search guidance: which user to move now, whether that move persists, and when a single move is insufficient so several must go together. The mechanistic separation already measured would carry over — today's probe found the third route changing **zero** proposed assignments while the first two change twelve to twenty-six and twenty-nine to sixty-nine, so they already enter through different paths.
    24	
    25	Two things would **not** survive:
    26	- the contract's identity fixing the sum of the first and third routes to the joint objective change, which exists to make coalition scores exact and is unnecessary for guidance;
    27	- the label distribution, because the target would be evaluated at every configuration the search visits rather than at an anchor's reference. **Whether the first route predicts well on that distribution has never been measured.**
    28	
    29	## The nearest precedent
    30	This is the learning-to-branch pattern that the external round already cited: learn a cheap surrogate for an expensive expert decision so the existing search runs faster, rather than replacing the search. Applied here it would let a learned component stand legitimately on the large margin, by producing it sooner rather than by improving on it.
    31	
    32	## Why nothing is being proposed
    33	Three reasons to measure before moving. The screening run will separate the cases: if all three routes show positive contributions where they are, nothing needs changing; if the **first** route alone shows nothing, that is evidence *for* this observation rather than against the route; if none of the three shows anything, the problem lies elsewhere and reattachment would not help.
    34	
    35	The screen is cheap and those three outcomes point to three different next steps. And a reattachment is a larger change than the one I declined earlier today for removing the three-route structure — it would rewrite the targets, the heads and three contract sections. It is not something to start on an argument.

     1	# Controller strategic observation — the energy is in the control law, not in the association
     2	Recorded 2026-09-10 after the owner said plainly that a one per cent improvement is not a meaningful result and asked for a direction that genuinely raises efficiency. This is **reasoning over today's measurements, not a new measurement**, and it is recorded as a direction to be decided, not started. `DIAGNOSTIC_NOT_CLAIM`.
     3	
     4	## The arithmetic that settles where the leverage is
     5	The power amplifier accounts for **94.8 %** of consumed power, and its draw is set by the **power and mode control law** — not by which user is attached to which beam.
     6	
     7	| what moved | pooled efficiency |
     8	|---|---|
     9	| **correcting one defect in the control law** | **28.47 → 43.71, about +53 %** |
    10	| everything the association layer can contend for — coordination, joint search, all three learned routes | **about +1 %** |
    11	
    12	**The largest efficiency movement measured today came from a control-law repair, not from any association decision.** And that repair only removed a defect; nobody has asked whether the law is well designed.
    13	
    14	## Why the association layer is limited to about one per cent
    15	It is not a weakness of the algorithm. Association decisions simply do not move much energy. Two local optima of the association search differ by about eight per cent; the coordinated optimum sits about one per cent above the certified fixed point. Both are small because they rearrange *who attaches where* while the energy is spent on *how much power each beam radiates and in which mode*.
    16	
    17	**The project has been looking for energy where the energy is not.**
    18	
    19	## This was predicted this morning and I did not act on it
    20	The strategic review's closing section asked whether the occupancy-induced blackout should exist at all, proposed comparing a service-aware power and mode policy against the existing law, and said: *if much of the coordination gain disappears, the contribution becomes a diagnosis of the control law and its remedy — possibly more useful than teaching three networks to compensate for it.*
    21	
    22	**That condition came true.** Correcting the provisioning took the coordination gain from 6.36 % to about 1 %. I spent the remaining hours pursuing the one per cent.
    23	
    24	## The option, stated with its costs
    25	The three-route structure survives a change of decision object. Pointed at the control law rather than the association, the roles map directly: the surplus of changing a beam's power and mode setting; whether that setting persists; and the interaction between beams' settings. **The interaction is stronger there**, because power settings couple directly through interference rather than indirectly through shared airtime, so the third route would have more to predict, not less.
    26	
    27	Three honest qualifications:
    28	1. **The +53 % was a defect repair, not an algorithmic gain.** It demonstrates that the control law has high leverage — a single defect there cost more than the entire association layer can win — but it is **not** fifty-three per cent sitting available.
    29	2. **The control law's improvable margin has never been measured**, exactly as the association layer's one per cent had never been measured before today.
    30	3. **It is a large pivot**: targets, heads and three contract sections would be rewritten.
    31	
    32	## What should happen before any decision
    33	The screening run is already committed and its cost already spent. It answers whether the three-head architecture functions at all, and that knowledge transfers to any decision object.
    34	
    35	Beside it, one measurement would answer the owner's question directly: **how far is the current power and mode control law from the best achievable, on the same panels?** That is the control-law analogue of the multi-start ceiling now being measured for the association layer, and nobody has ever asked it.
    36	
    37	**Proposed, not started.** The owner was told the reason it is necessary rather than merely interesting, and the decision is theirs.
