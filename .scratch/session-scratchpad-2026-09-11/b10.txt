    43

    46	configurations by that residual **while ignoring the per-user terms it is a residual of** is
    47	not an information source being used on its own; it is a fragment of an identity evaluated
    48	outside the identity.
    49	
    50	So `C3_ONLY > BASELINE` is a requirement the present decomposition's third route is
    51	**structurally poorly placed to meet** — not because the coordination information is absent,
    52	but because the object chosen to carry it is defined by subtraction. Its measured `-0.06%` is
    53	consistent with that: near zero, slightly the wrong side.

    56	against the owner's ask.** Both candidates on record define three routes each of which is a
    57	standalone quantity:
    58

    60	  candidate's activation; continuation. Pointwise parts-to-total `1.00x` at small sizes,
    61	  `1.61x` at the grand row, against the incumbent's `1.57x`-`22.99x`.
    62	- **A** (astra): local-link, temporal and relational innovations of `E[Y | I0, X_J]` where
