     1	# Feasibility — what would have to be true, checked against what is achieved
     2	
     3	**2026-09-10 11:20 UTC. Arithmetic only, on receipts already on disk. No new compute.**
     4	This calculation had never been done. Roughly 100 controller records and 80 KB of adversarial
     5	audit contain no error budget, no required-accuracy figure, and no power statement for the
     6	requirement as it now stands. Every question asked so far has been "what happened when I
     7	measured X", never "what must be true for this to work at all".
     8	
     9	---
    10	
    11	## 1. Required accuracy of the heads
    12	
    13	The composed score at `|A| = 100` is `sum_i d_i + Psi = +480.113 - 440.079 = +40.034`
    14	*(`SELECTOR-DIAGNOSIS-2026-09-09.md` §3, normalised score/kappa units)*.
    15	
    16	- Worst-case amplification `(|S| + |R|) / |V| = 22.985x`. A **1%** component error becomes up
    17	  to **23%** error on the quantity actually ranked; **5%** becomes **115%**.
    18	- The margin that must survive: the best single-user move is `8.02` against a summed term of
    19	  `200.4` over 82 users *(`CORPUS-IS-SURROGATE` §2)*. So a per-user error above roughly
    20	  **1.67%** of the summed term can reorder the top pair.
    21	
    22	**Required per-user relative accuracy is therefore on the order of 1%, and better where the
    23	top two catalogue rows are close.**
    24	
    25	## 2. Achieved accuracy
    26	
    27	`C1REAL`, declared target, exact path, held out:
    28	
    29	| | sealed `(8,)` head | flexible model, same inputs | floor |
    30	|---|---:|---:|---:|
    31	| LOAO level `R^2` | `-0.2724` | `0.0933` | — |
    32	| LOWO level `R^2` | — | `-0.0714` | — |
    33	| top-1 | `0.1711` | `0.1854` | `0.1716` |
    34	
    35	**A negative held-out `R^2` means the predictor is worse than the target's own mean.** Its
    36	relative error exceeds **100%** of the target's spread.
    37	
    38	**Required ~1%. Achieved >100%. That is roughly two orders of magnitude, and it is a property
    39	of the target and the inputs, not of the optimiser.** A schema repair is running; it changes
    40	effective dimension from 12 to 14. It would have to close two orders of magnitude.
    41	
    42	## 3. Detectability of the required contrasts
    43	
    44	| source | fact |
    45	|---|---|
    46	| contract v1 §, 2026-09-08 | a true **+1%** is **not detectable** with ~160 dates **under any seed count** if the date SD is ~5% |
    47	| contract v1.1 amendment, 2026-09-09 | measured **three**-contrast conjunction power `0.675` at `delta* = +2%`, 16 seeds, 160 dates |
    48	| requirement, 2026-09-10 08:55 | **six** contrasts, not three |
    49	| corrected coordination band | `+0.899%` (8 anchors) / `+1.29%` (20 anchors) |
    50	| archive erratum, 2026-09-09 | **166** TRAIN dates available against ~160 needed — **1.04x** headroom |
    51	| `FINDING-ONE-DATE` | the learner has **D = 1** date; "at fixed D, adding seeds drives the contrast variance to `Sigma_date/D` and no further, **so more seeds cannot help**" |
    52	| `learner.py:40` | the code runs **12** seeds; v1.1 requires **16**. This silently under-powers every conjunction test |
    53	
    54	**Arithmetic.** If three contrasts conjoin at power `0.675`, a per-contrast power of
    55	`0.675^(1/3) = 0.877` gives a six-contrast conjunction of `0.456` **under independence**. The
    56	six contrasts share arms and are positively correlated, so the true figure is **higher than
    57	`0.456` and lower than `0.675`** — the exact value is not derivable from the receipts, and I
    58	do not claim it.
    59	
    60	**That approximation is not load-bearing.** The direct statement is: the project's own contract
    61	says `+1%` is undetectable with this archive **under any seed count**, and the band being
    62	chased is `+0.899%` to `+1.29%`.
    63	
    64	## 4. The conclusion this forces
    65	
    66	**Two independent walls, either of which is sufficient:**
    67	
    68	1. **Accuracy.** The heads must be ~1% accurate on a per-user quantity they currently predict
    69	   worse than its own mean.
    70	2. **Detectability.** Even with perfect heads, an effect in the `+0.9%` to `+1.3%` band cannot
    71	   be established as significant with 166 dates, by the project's own power analysis — and the
    72	   learner currently has one date.
    73	
    74	**Neither wall is moved by fixing the corpus, repairing the schema, changing the decomposition,
    75	or training longer.** Wall 1 is about the information in the inputs versus the margin in the
    76	ranking. Wall 2 is about the archive versus the effect size.
    77	
    78	## 5. What this does not say
    79	
    80	- It does **not** say the mechanism is absent. The corrected coordination band is positive and
    81	  measured: `+0.899%` and `+1.29%`.
    82	- It does **not** say the heads are useless. Ordering `0.5889`, held out, is above chance.
    83	- It does **not** settle whether a different decomposition has a larger band — but the band is
    84	  a property of the physics, and the price-of-anarchy reading already put it near one per cent.
    85	- It does **not** authorise abandoning anything. It states what would have to be true.
    86	
    87	## 6. The choices this leaves, stated without preference
    88	
    89	1. **Enlarge the effect.** Find a regime or an action space where the coordination band is
    90	   materially larger than one per cent. The regime map has been swept and was non-monotone and
    91	   below one per cent everywhere tested, including at 200 users and 80 Mbit/s.
    92	2. **Enlarge the archive.** Generate more independent dates. `FINDING-ONE-DATE` names world
    93	   generation as the longest-lead item, and it is blocked on an owner decision open since
    94	   2026-09-09 that has not been re-raised. **This is the only wall-2 remedy that exists.**
    95	3. **Change the claim** to one the evidence can carry: the exact ladder with intervals, the
    96	   learned surrogate as *acceleration* of search against a stated deadline, the set-level
    97	   correction reported at its measured size, and the negative results as negative results.
    98	4. **Change the estimand** so the ranked quantity is not a near-cancelling difference. Two
    99	   candidate decompositions exist; whether either enlarges the band is unmeasured.
   100	
   101	**Options 1 and 2 are experiments. Option 3 is a decision. Option 4 is a design change.** None
   102	of them is training, and training does not become informative until at least one is settled.

     2	
     3	**2026-09-10 11:40 UTC. Written before the measurement exists.** No sealed constant,
     4	threshold, sign, seed, horizon, price, guard or acceptance rule is changed. This fixes what
     5	will be measured and how it will be read, while no number exists to tune against.
     6	
     7	---
     8	
     9	## 1. Why the axis changes, and why that is not outcome selection
    10	
    11	The claim under the current framing is that each route raises pooled EE **above the certified
    12	unilateral fixed point**. The size of that prize is measured, on the declared full-buffer
    13	numerator, and it is the ceiling for that claim regardless of how it is partitioned:
    14	
    15	| measurement | value | verification |
    16	|---|---|---|
    17	| 8 anchors, STRICT, correctly paired, 1.66° | `+0.899421%` | **controller-verified**, `final-strict-one-sided-1.66.json`, `summary.pairings.correct.pooled_relative_ee_gain` = `0.008994207878970206` |
    18	| 30 TRAIN dates, `MARGIN_Q`, date-paired | mean `+0.717%`, **date SD `0.751%`**, 95% t `[+0.436%, +0.997%]`, **4/30 non-positive** | **controller-verified**, `CEILING30-MARGIN-2026-09-10.md:34` |
    19	| load/rate regime sweep, 11 points | never above `1%` | reported, not re-verified |
    20	
    21	**The owner has stated that a ~1% result is not meaningful.** Partitioning ≤1% three ways
    22	cannot produce three substantial contributions. That is arithmetic, and it was established
    23	**before** this pre-declaration and independently of any route's sign.
    24	
    25	**The rationale for changing axis is therefore outcome-independent**: the prize on the current
    26	axis is too small for the claim, whichever way it comes out. It is not "the routes failed here
    27	so try elsewhere"; the ceiling was measured, and no partition of it can meet the stated bar.
    28	
    29	## 2. The axis, and that the contract already names it
    30	
    31	**Contract v1 F2, controller-verified:** *"the coordinator's compute budget is **10 s wall** on
    32	the declared worker count (the rest of the 30.08 s interval reserved for
    33	sensing/transport/validation/commit); BASE (a⁰) is computed, validated and repaired **before**
    34	the coordinator starts; the runner enforces the timer (cancel), not the solver; a miss executes
    35	the validated a⁰."*
    36	
    37	**Contract v1 C4, controller-verified:** *"matching S0 at lower compute is an **acceleration
    38	claim, reported separately**."*
    39	
    40	**The measured prize on this axis** (`ANYTIME-UNILATERAL-2026-09-10.md`, `SEALED` pooled curve,
    41	controller-verified):
    42	
    43	| budget | anytime EE (Mbit/J) | fraction of the first-improvement prize |
    44	|---:|---:|---:|
    45	| 10 s (the contract's coordinator budget) | **15.336646** | **42.91%** |
    46	| 30.08 s (the whole anchor interval) | 25.846321 | 81.74% |
    47	| convergence | **30.786890** | 100% |
    48	
    49	**At the contract's own budget the headroom above the anytime incumbent is `+100.7%`**, two
    50	orders of magnitude larger than the coordination band.
    51	
    52	**The receipt's own warning, which this pre-declaration adopts:** the comparator is *"the
    53	actual anytime incumbent at the same wall-clock budget, not the legal base configuration or a
    54	convergence certificate time."* Any comparison against `BASE` on this axis is void.
    55	
    56	## 3. What is to be measured
    57	
    58	For every arm in the sealed inventory — `FULL`, `DROP_C1`, `DROP_C2`, `DROP_C3`,
    59	`ALL_NEUTRAL_CONTROL` — at the contract's 10 s coordinator budget, on the same anchors, same
    60	catalogue construction, same guard, same tie-break, same nominal information class (A2: no
    61	realised fading), reporting on the **declared full-buffer numerator**:
    62	
    63	1. **Recovered fraction.** Pooled EE attained, as a fraction of the certified fixed point's
    64	   pooled EE on the same anchors.
    65	2. **The contrasts.** `FULL - ALL_NEUTRAL_CONTROL`, and `FULL - DROP_Ci` for each route, on
    66	   recovered fraction. **`ALL_NEUTRAL_CONTROL` is the reference. `BASE` is not, and is never
    67	   relabelled as one.**
    68	3. **The comparator.** The anytime incumbent's recovered fraction at the same 10 s budget,
    69	   reported beside every arm.
    70	4. **Deadline misses.** Per contract F2 a miss executes the validated `a⁰`; misses are a
    71	   co-reported QoS field and their B and E enter the endpoint. Report the miss rate per arm.
    72	5. **Physics evaluations consumed**, per arm, so "acceleration" has a denominator.
    73	
    74	## 4. The reading, fixed now
    75	
    76	- **The claim is about recovered fraction under budget, not about EE above the fixed point.**
    77	  No figure from this measurement may be restated as a coordination gain, and no coordination
    78	  figure may be restated as an acceleration gain.
    79	- **`FULL > ALL_NEUTRAL_CONTROL` on recovered fraction is the primary contrast.** If it fails,
    80	  the learned system does not beat neutral-source training on this axis either, and that is
    81	  reported as the result.
    82	- **`FULL > DROP_Ci` for each route is the per-route contrast.** Whatever ordering emerges
    83	  among the three is reported as measured; **no ordering among routes is required**, because
    84	  the relative importance of the routes is an empirical fact and not a design target.
    85	- **A route whose contrast is zero or negative is reported as zero or negative.** One or two
    86	  small contributors is an outcome, not a failure to be repaired by adjustment.
    87	- **If the anytime incumbent at the same budget beats every learned arm, that is the result**,
    88	  and the acceleration claim fails with it.
    89	- **Three routes are not assumed necessary here.** If a single learned proposer recovers as
    90	  much as `FULL`, that is a finding about the decomposition and must be reported, not
    91	  suppressed.
    92	
    93	## 5. What this pre-declaration does not do
    94	
    95	- It does not authorise training, a panel, contract v2, or a corpus substitution.
    96	- It does not claim the acceleration axis will succeed. **Nothing on this axis has been
    97	  measured for any learned arm.**
    98	- It does not retract the coordination result. `+0.899%` / `+0.717%` stand as measured, with
    99	  their interval and their 4/30 non-positive dates, and remain reportable as a mechanism-level
   100	  finding.
   101	- It does not move any pass mark. The owner's bar — substantial, not ~1% — is unchanged; what
   102	  changes is which quantity it is applied to, and why.
