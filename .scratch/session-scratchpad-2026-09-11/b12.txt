     9	## 0. Why this run exists
    10	
    11	**No production training run scored on the reported endpoint has ever happened in this
    12	project.** Every EE figure quoted today is from an oracle or an exact search:
    13	
    14	| figure | source | learner involved |

    20	The one measurement involving trained heads is `C3REACH`, which used four epoch-2000 `FULL`
    21	lineages and reported pooled EE `36.63 -> 15.84` Mbit/J under the pilot's boundary-0 scoring —

    25	**So the quantity every argument today has turned on — what a trained system actually does to

    31	Before training, on a checked-in fixture, demonstrate that `FULL`, each `DROP_Ci` and

    33	PASS/FAIL per pair. **A FAIL stops the run.** This gate exists because `MARGIN_Q` produced
    34	eight contrasts identical to 16 significant digits, and the C2 arms in `FACTORIAL` produced
    35	three byte-identical pairs, and neither had such a fixture.

    52	
    53	- **Reference: `ALL_NEUTRAL_CONTROL`.** `BASE` is **not** a reference on this run and is never

    62	- **Panel:** small and stated before the run — anchors, worlds, seeds, and the exact anchor
    63	  list, fixed in the launch receipt. **This is a signal, not a claim panel.** No result from
    64	  this run is a claim about efficacy.

    66	## 4. The continuation rule, fixed now
    67	
    68	Continue past 500 **only** if the primary contrast is still **moving monotonically across the
    69	five checkpoints**, i.e. not yet converged.
    70	
    71	**This condition is about convergence, not direction.** If the contrast has flattened — at any
    72	value, positive, zero or negative — the run stops at 500 and is reported as it stands. I will
    73	not continue because a number is disappointing, and I will not stop because a number is
    74	pleasing.
    75	
    76	## 5. The reading, fixed now
    77	
    78	- **`FULL - ALL_NEUTRAL_CONTROL` negative or zero at convergence**: the learned system does not
    79	  beat neutral-source training on this corpus, schema and panel. That is the result. It is
    80	  reported with the same detail as any other, and it does **not** license a further design
    81	  change chosen to reverse it.
    82	- **A route's `FULL - DROP_Ci` zero or negative**: reported as zero or negative. No ordering
    83	  among routes is required.
    84	- **A flat-zero contrast at 500 with no movement across checkpoints** is evidence the heads
    85	  learned nothing usable on this corpus — a different finding from a converged negative, and
    86	  reported as such.
    87	- **Loss values are never evidence about EE**, in either direction, at any checkpoint.
    88	- **Every reported number carries four fields**: reference, information class, estimand,
