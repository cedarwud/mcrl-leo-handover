     1	# Declaration — the MODQN comparator binding, and an amendment to a sealed condition
     2	
     3	Date: 2026-09-10 ~17:05Z
     4	Decision by: the owner, on this date, in response to `BASELINE-MODQN-REFERENCE-2026-09-10.md`
     5	Status: **the acceptance gate's definition. Written before any MODQN EE on V0.25 exists.**
     6	
     7	## The gap this closes
     8	
     9	`BASELINE-MODQN-REFERENCE-2026-09-10.md` (`/home/sat/mcrl-v025-modqn-ws`) established, by
    10	reading the seals and loading the artefacts:
    11	
    12	- the V0.25 contract names an **"external BASELINE"** and **binds nothing executable** — no
    13	  policy, checkpoint, training regime, or V0.25-to-legacy interface mapping;
    14	- the V0.23 declaration binds it precisely: *"the unchanged, externally authenticated
    15	  pre-Catfish MODQN policy … and is **never a trained arm**"*, checkpoint SHA-256
    16	  `e6b063efea8608fd1e46ac15d5442aa8d1171dffc9f0b5e8cc209eca1b09c28b`, **112 states, 28
    17	  actions**, 9,000 episodes;
    18	- the checkpoint and its adapter still load, but **no path submits their decisions to the
    19	  V0.25 `a-r0` evaluator**, so the gate is **unmeasured, not zero**;
    20	- the object the current pilot calls `BASELINE` is `carrier_base`, a **fixed carrier geometry
    21	  control**. **Using its numbers as the MODQN gate would be a category error**, and without
    22	  this determination that is what tonight would have reported.
    23	
    24	## The decision
    25	
    26	**Primary gate: a MODQN retrained on V0.25 physics.**
    27	**Secondary, reported alongside: the frozen V0.23 checkpoint replayed on V0.25 physics.**
    28	
    29	Both are reported, always, in every place the gate is stated. Neither may be omitted because
    30	it is the less favourable of the two.
    31	
    32	**Why retrained is primary.** The V0.23 checkpoint learned on a physics engine that V0.25
    33	replaced — angle/rate/TPC/TDM/ACM, corrected v1.9 provisioning, a different energy account.
    34	A policy frozen on the old engine and deployed on the new one is handicapped by distribution
    35	shift. Beating it would partly measure *"we changed the engine"*, not *"the three routes
    36	help"*. The owner's criterion is about the three routes, so the gate must be physics-matched.
    37	
    38	**Why frozen is retained as secondary.** It is what the sealed record literally names, and
    39	dropping it would break continuity with the V0.23 declaration.
    40	
    41	## This amends a sealed condition, and the amendment is declared as such
    42	
    43	The V0.23 declaration says `BASELINE` **"is never a trained arm"**. Retraining it is a change
    44	to that condition, so it is declared, not slipped in:
    45	
    46	1. **The prior declaration is acknowledged**, quoted above in full.
    47	2. **The reason is independent of the outcome it would help.** V0.25 replaced the physics
    48	   engine; a comparator frozen on the previous engine is not like-for-like. That reason existed
    49	   before any V0.25 measurement and does not depend on which way the comparison goes.
    50	3. **The outcome is not known at the time of the decision.** `MODQNREF` verified that MODQN's
    51	   V0.25 pooled EE is **unmeasured**. Nobody — owner or controller — knows whether retraining
    52	   makes the gate easier or harder.
    53	4. **The frozen arm is not removed**, only demoted to secondary, so no evidence is lost.
    54	
    55	## Fairness bindings — fixed now, before MODQN is trained
    56	
    57	Retraining creates a surface on which the comparator could be weakened, deliberately or by
    58	inattention. These close it. **They are fixed before the run and are not to be revised against
    59	an observed MODQN result.**
    60	
    61	1. **At least the Catfish budget.** MODQN receives **no less** training budget and **no fewer
    62	   seeds** than the five sealed Catfish arms. If the Catfish arms run 16 seeds, MODQN runs at
    63	   least 16.
    64	2. **At least the Catfish information.** MODQN's observation carries **no less** decision-time
    65	   information than the Catfish system's. If a field is available to Q1/Q2/Q3, it is available
    66	   to MODQN unless the legacy interface structurally cannot carry it — and every such case is
    67	   named in the report.
    68	3. **Its own hyperparameters, honestly chosen.** MODQN is tuned by the **same procedure** used
    69	   for the Catfish heads — a convergence screen against a rule fixed before it runs, computing
    70	   no EE — and the admissible setting is chosen on the same grounds (earliest admissibility,
    71	   then cost). It is not given the Catfish heads' literals if those are wrong for it.
    72	4. **No outcome-selected reruns.** The first completed run under this binding is the reported
    73	   one. A rerun requires a stated defect, declared before its result is seen.
    74	5. **Report the stronger of primary and secondary as the harder gate.** If the frozen replay
    75	   scores **higher** than the retrained MODQN, the frozen number is the one the Catfish must
    76	   clear. **The comparator is never selected downward.**
    77	6. **Configuration frozen before launch**, with digests, in a launch receipt.
    78	
    79	## The evaluator rule, binding on every measurement under this declaration
    80	
    81	Selection: **one fresh dense `StepEvaluator(boundary_indices=(0,))`** per anchor, with `BASE`
    82	and every candidate in its **first** `evaluate_many` call; **no scalar `evaluate`**, no foreign
    83	cache preceding it. Endpoints: **one separate fresh realised dense full-48** evaluator, `BASE`
    84	and all endpoints in one call. Assert both in code and report the assertion result.
    85	
    86	Non-negotiable: `SELECTION-SURFACE-2026-09-10.md` showed a scalar-cached `BASE` moved a
    87	published fixed point from `13.430253` to `31.028111`, retaining **0 of 12** configuration IDs.
    88	
    89	## What is measured, and how it is reported
    90	
    91	On the development panel, full-buffer numerator, no demand cap: pooled EE, pooled bits, pooled
    92	joules, served PHY, rate-target attainment, `modal_frac`, `active` beams and satellites,
    93	`argmax_distinct` — for **retrained MODQN**, **frozen-replay MODQN**, and the learned arms.
    94	
    95	**Disclosure that is not optional.** The non-learned family is **not** a gate — the owner has
    96	fixed that the Catfish need only beat MODQN. But where the Catfish and MODQN sit relative to
    97	`RSS_MAX` (**41.621560**), the clean fixed point (**31.028111**) and `RANDOM` (**11.233999**)
    98	is **reported every time the gate is reported**. A reviewer will ask; the answer is given
    99	before it is asked.
   100	
   101	## Open, and not settled here
   102	
   103	Whether MODQN retraining on V0.25 is a bounded piece of work at all. `MODQNBRIDGE` (17:03Z) is
   104	building the option-independent half — the 112-field projection, the 28-slot roster with a
   105	physical-identity round trip, the edge rules and known-answer tests — and is instructed to stop
   106	and report if the projection **cannot be completed without invention**. If it cannot, this
   107	declaration's primary gate is not yet constructible and that becomes the next decision.
=================== V025-CONTROLLER-DECLARATION-RUNNER-V2-2026-09-10.md
     1	# Declaration — runner v2: raise the epoch cap, add the declared decay, keep v1 byte-identical
     2	
     3	Date: 2026-09-10 ~17:22Z · Controller
     4	**Written before any EE exists on the exact corpus. No EE quantity has been computed from it.**
     5	
     6	## What blocked
     7	
     8	`EXACT-CORPUS-TRAINING-2026-09-10.md` (`/home/sat/mcrl-v025-exacttrain-ws`) assembled the exact
     9	corpus and passed every label check, then **stopped before creating an output directory**:
    10	
    11	> the required runner, SHA-256 `4885705…c797d66`, accepts only **1–2,000 epochs** and implements
    12	> the legacy **constant-rate, exact-budget/no-early-selection** contract. It cannot express the
    13	> declared 4,000-update step decay and cadence-100 stability stopping rule without changing the
    14	> sealed implementation or inventing a new runner and held-out split.
    15	
    16	`DECAY` measured first-admissibility for step decay at **2,200 updates**. The cap is 2,000.
    17	**It is short by 200.**
    18	
    19	## The three-layer test, applied
    20	
    21	- **Scientific sealing?** No. `V025-CONTROLLER-WHAT-IS-ACTUALLY-SEALED-2026-09-10.md` lists the
    22	  **2000-epoch cap** explicitly among the things that are **not sealed**, alongside lr,
    23	  `gauge_beta`, hidden layers, activation, Adam betas, checkpoint cadence and catalogue caps.
    24	  That listing was written this morning, before any of today's results.
    25	- **Compatibility pinning?** Yes. Three completed runs carry launch receipts binding
    26	  `4885705…c797d66`. **Runner v1 stays byte-identical and those receipts remain valid.**
    27	- **Implementation default?** Yes, and the rule for those is: **declare before the judging
    28	  measurement.** This is that declaration, and no EE from the exact corpus exists yet.
    29	
    30	## Authorised
    31	
    32	**Runner v2, as a new file with a new digest. v1 is not edited.**
    33	
    34	1. **Epoch cap `1–4,000`.** The 2,000 literal was an implementation default, never a scientific
    35	   choice.
    36	2. **Step-decay schedule support**, expressing the already-declared schedule
    37	   (`V025-CONTROLLER-DECLARATION-SCHEDULE-CHOICE-2026-09-10`): from `1e-3`, factor `0.1` after
    38	   2,000 updates and again after 3,000. **Constant rate remains available and remains the
    39	   default**, so v2 can reproduce v1's behaviour exactly.
    40	3. **Nothing else changes.** Not the architecture, not the arms, not the corpus contract, not
    41	   the fixture gate, not the checkpoint format, not the cadence semantics.
    42	
    43	**A v1-equivalence test is required**: run v2 with a constant rate on the same corpus and seed
    44	and show it reproduces a v1 checkpoint **bit-for-bit**. Without that, v2 is not usable, because
    45	a silent behavioural change would be indistinguishable from a result.
    46	
    47	## Not authorised
    48	
    49	**Do not put the stopping rule inside the runner.** The runner already writes cadence-100
    50	checkpoints; the pre-declared stability criterion is applied to that checkpoint sequence
    51	**offline**, exactly as `DECAY` and `HORIZON` applied it. Adding a held-out split and
    52	early-selection to the training loop would change the runner's exact-budget contract, and that
    53	**is** a scientific change — it would let the training loop select against held-out data.
    54	
    55	**Do not rescale the decay schedule to fit a 2,000 cap.** Compressing the decay points to
    56	1,000/1,500 because 2,200 exceeds 2,000 would be choosing a schedule against a constraint
    57	discovered after the schedule was declared. Raise the cap instead; it is the free variable.
    58	
    59	## Why this is not the move it might resemble
    60	
    61	I have attacked sealed declarations three times this project on bad grounds. This is not that:
    62	the object being changed was **listed as unsealed this morning, in writing, before any of
    63	today's measurements**; the change is a version, not an edit; v1 remains valid for the runs
    64	that used it; and the declaration precedes the measurement it affects. If any of those four
    65	were untrue I would not authorise it.

=================== V025-CONTROLLER-RULING-THREE-BINDINGS-2026-09-10.md
     1	# Ruling — the three route bindings, and two proposals I am not adopting as written
     2	
     3	Date: 2026-09-10 ~17:40Z · Controller
     4	Source: `APPROACHING-THE-INSTRUMENTS-2026-09-10.md` (`/home/sat/mcrl-v025-approach-ws`),
     5	verified over **all 16** `FULL` epoch-500 checkpoints, 12 development anchors, with the
     6	evaluator rule asserted in code (scalar `evaluate` replaced by a raising stub, **zero** calls,
     7	24/24 assertions passed, instrument endpoints reproduced to `rel_tol=1e-12`).
     8	
     9	## The findings I accept as established
    10	
    11	1. **Neither Q1 v2 nor Q2 v2 carries per-legal-option `nominal_gain`, nor primitives sufficient
    12	   to reconstruct it.** The physical RSS action is present in **1,200/1,200** user-anchor action
    13	   tables. The action is available; the value that would rank it is not.
    14	2. **Median `a0`-to-`RSS_MAX` Hamming distance is 92.5 of 100 users.**
    15	3. **Against `RSS_MAX`, every differing learned choice has lower nominal gain.** Systematic, not
    16	   a tie-breaking effect.
    17	4. **Against the crowded endpoint, 99.13% of differing learned choices land on a less-occupied
    18	   beam**, and that endpoint differs on a median 93 users — far outside a catalogue that is
    19	   98.13% `|A| <= 2`.
    20	5. **Bindings: C1 = features, C2 = target, C3 = candidates. Optimisation is secondary but real
    21	   in all three** (the sealed head loses to plain linear on level calibration in every route).
    22	
    23	## C1 — ADOPT the feature addition, as a successor
    24	
    25	Add one per-legal-option `decision_boundary_log_nominal_gain_db` scalar to the Q1 action row.
    26	
    27	**Why this is not handing the head the answer.** `RSS_MAX` is a demand-blind greedy. A head
    28	that can see gain is *able* to reproduce it but is not obliged to; the whole point of a learned
    29	method is to use gain where gain decides and to deviate where demand, occupancy or interaction
    30	decide. Removing a blindfold is not the same as supplying a policy.
    31	
    32	**What I will not claim.** I have no evidence that the omission was an oversight rather than a
    33	design choice, and I am not asserting it was. The schema carries other quantities; it does not
    34	carry this one. That is the whole finding.
    35	
    36	**Consequence, declared:** a new Q1 schema width and digest, a new corpus, new checkpoints, and
    37	therefore a **successor learned arm**. The sealed five-arm inventory is **not** mutated in
    38	place. `C3 = 1 + 2*(2*Q1 + 6) + 4*7 + (32*4 + 1) + 6` moves with Q1, and the derivation is
    39	enforced fail-closed.
    40	
    41	## C2 — HOLD, with a stated objection to the proposal as written
    42	
    43	Proposed: redefine the label as *incremental future value after conditioning on / subtracting
    44	the current-slot C1 contribution*.
    45	
    46	**The objection.** Defining C2's target as "the residual C1 did not capture" puts the
    47	decomposition into the structure where **a positive C2 marginal is least informative**, because
    48	the target is constructed to be whatever the other route missed. It is not guaranteed positive
    49	— the residual may be unlearnable or worth nothing in EE — but if it comes out positive I would
    50	be unable to distinguish "C2 carries real horizon value" from "C2 fits C1's leftovers".
    51	
    52	**This is not a rejection.** The report's sequencing argument is sound: while C1 cannot see
    53	gain, C2's absolute persistence label is partly spent re-deriving the current choice, and the
    54	two routes compete instead of composing.
    55	
    56	**Ruling: hold C2's redefinition until C1's feature change has been made and measured.** Then
    57	decide with the residual's actual size in view, and **declare in advance how a positive C2
    58	marginal under a residual target would be distinguished from construction** — before that
    59	number exists. If no such distinction can be stated, the redefinition is not adopted.
    60	
    61	## C3 — ADOPT in principle, but not before `CEILING2` reports
    62	
    63	Proposed: add one deterministic whole-profile `RSS_MAX` proposal to the bounded catalogue.
    64	
    65	**Why it survives the obvious objection.** The row is offered to **every** arm, including
    66	`DROP_C3` and `ALL_NEUTRAL_CONTROL`. It raises the floor for all of them, so the `FULL` minus
    67	`DROP_C3` contrast still isolates C3's contribution rather than measuring the row. Putting a
    68	known-good profile on the menu is not the same as putting it in one arm's pocket.
    69	
    70	**What it does change, and must be reported:** the meaning of every ceiling computed over that
    71	catalogue. A "coordination ceiling" measured with an `RSS_MAX` row present is a different
    72	quantity from one measured without it, and the two must never be compared.
    73	
    74	**Ruling: hold until `CEILING2` reports.** Its Part 2 is already sweeping catalogue coalition
    75	support (`|A| <= 1, 2, 3, 4, 6`). If the ceiling **saturates** at `|A| = 2`, the candidate set
    76	is not C3's binding constraint and this proposal is answering the wrong question. If it keeps
    77	rising, add the row **and** report the support sweep beside it, since a single hand-chosen
    78	profile and a genuinely wider support are different claims.
    79	
    80	## Optimisation — a control, not a fix
    81	
    82	After each binding is released, the **first** thing run is a calibrated linear head on the same
    83	features, as the report recommends. The sealed head currently loses to plain linear on level
    84	calibration in all three routes; until a change beats that control, "the head learned it" is
    85	not established.
    86	
    87	**A calibrated linear control cannot create absent gain information or absent configurations.**
    88	So it is a control on the optimisation claim only, never a substitute for the feature or
    89	candidate change.
    90	
    91	## Sequence
    92	
    93	1. **C1's feature addition** — the largest lever and the prerequisite for C2's question being
    94	   well-posed. Held only until `EXACTTRAIN2` proves the exact-corpus path end to end, so the
    95	   new corpus is built once on a proven pipeline rather than twice.
    96	2. **`CEILING2` reports** → then rule on C3's catalogue row.
    97	3. **C2's target** — last, and only with a pre-declared test that separates real horizon value
    98	   from residual fitting.
    99	
   100	## Every one of these creates a successor arm
   101	
   102	None mutates the sealed five-arm inventory. Each is an owner-visible declared design change
   103	with new schema digests, new corpora and new checkpoints, and each is recorded before it is
   104	measured. The current five arms remain exactly as sealed, and the runs already completed
   105	against them remain valid for what they measured.
