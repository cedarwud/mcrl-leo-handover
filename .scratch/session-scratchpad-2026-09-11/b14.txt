   140	
   141	`HORIZON` runs one seed and one route both ways, applies LRSWEEP's existing rule to both, and

=================== V025-CONTROLLER-RULING-C3-ENCODER-CONTRACT-2026-09-10.md
     1	# Ruling — the C3 encoder width contract
     2	
     3	Date: 2026-09-10 ~16:22Z · Controller
     4	Proposal from: `PANELFIX`, in its stop-for-authorisation output
     5	Ruling: **ADOPT THE DERIVATION NOW, DEFER THE FORMAT CHANGE**
     6	
     7	## The defect
     8	
     9	Four mutually incompatible C3 context widths exist simultaneously, and **no file declares one
    10	authoritatively**:
    11	
    12	| producer | C1 | C2 | C3 |
    13	|---|---:|---:|---:|
    14	| v1 run = production encoder (Q1 v1) | 16 | 22 | **240** |
    15	| v2 run (Q1 v2) | 15 | 22 | **236** |
    16	| z-view run (Q1 v2 + cross-user z) | 30 | 44 | **296** |
    17	| the panel PANELBUILD shipped | 16 | 22 | **237** |
    18	
    19	**237 is a builder defect**, not a schema: `build_stagec_scoring_panel.py:147` truncated the
    20	six-field ordered global suffix to three fields and did not fail. The other three are correct
    21	consequences of their Q1 schemas.
    22	
    23	**Both sides derive the width from checkpoint weights and compare against whatever the panel
    24	happens to contain.** Neither consults a declared constant. That is why the drift was silent
    25	and why it surfaced only at scoring time.
    26	
    27	## What is already established, and is stronger than the proposal assumed
    28	
    29	The width is **not** an arbitrary per-producer choice. It is a function:
    30	
    31	```
    32	member_width = 2 * Q1_width + 6
    33	C3_width     = 1 + 2 * member_width + 4*7 + (32*4 + 1) + 6
    34	```
    35	
    36	Verified against **three independent real checkpoints**: `Q1=16 -> 240`, `Q1=15 -> 236`,
    37	`Q1=30 -> 296`. The z case was **predicted before it was measured** and matched exactly.
    38	
    39	**A contract does not need to be stored to be checkable — it needs to be derivable and
    40	enforced.** That changes the ruling.
    41	
    42	## Ruling
    43	
    44	**1. ADOPT, effective immediately, as validation rather than format.**
    45	
    46	Every producer and every consumer of a C3 state vector must, before use, derive the expected
    47	width from the Q1 schema in hand and **fail closed** on mismatch. No checkpoint field, no panel
    48	field, no format version is required for this. It can be added today, to code that is already
    49	running, without touching the three live runs.
    50	
    51	**2. ADOPT the fail-closed builder fix.** Already authorised to `PANELFIX2`: silent truncation
    52	is the defect that produced 237. A build that now fails was always wrong.
    53	
    54	**3. DEFER the stored-digest half of the proposal to the next training generation.**
    55	
    56	Storing an authenticated encoder-contract digest in both checkpoint and panel, and comparing
    57	digests before scoring, is the right end state. **It is a checkpoint format change, and three
    58	16-seed runs are writing checkpoints in the current format right now** (landing ~17:05Z,
    59	~17:20Z, ~18:20Z). Changing the format today would strand them.
    60	
    61	It is declared here as the **next generation's** format, to be applied when a training run is
    62	next launched from a clean start — not retrofitted.
    63	
    64	**4. REJECT one-panel-per-schema as the permanent answer.** `PANELZ` is asked whether one panel
    65	carrying a per-schema state block can serve all widths. If it can, that is the target: the
    66	20-anchor **physical** content is schema-independent — the catalogue, the full-buffer outcomes,
    67	the fixed point, the anytime incumbent — and only the state encoding varies. Rebuilding
    68	identical physics per schema is waste and is itself a drift surface.
    69	
    70	## Why this is engineering, not science
    71	
    72	**No number changes.** No estimand, reference, numerator or arm definition is affected. This
    73	governs whether two artefacts may be compared at all, not what the comparison says. It touches
    74	no sealed declaration and requires no versioned model amendment.
    75	
    76	## The general rule this instance is an example of
    77	
    78	**A quantity that three producers compute independently and no file declares will drift, and it
    79	will surface at the most expensive step.** Here it surfaced at scoring, an hour before three
    80	trainings landed, and only because the scorer was run against a real checkpoint instead of
    81	being trusted.
    82	
    83	Where a quantity is **derivable**, enforce the derivation at every boundary and fail closed.
    84	Where it is **chosen**, one file declares it and everyone reads that file. What must not happen
    85	is what happened here: everyone infers it from whatever they were handed.
