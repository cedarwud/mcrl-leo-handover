     1	# Pre-declaration — head-to-head screen of the two candidate decompositions
     2	
     3	**2026-09-10, written BEFORE any exact-path row exists.** `EXACTGEN` is generating the
     4	corpus and has produced nothing yet. No sealed artefact, constant, threshold, sign, seed,
     5	horizon, price, guard or acceptance rule is changed. This fixes the comparison metrics while
     6	no data exists to tune them against.
     7	
     8	## The two candidates
     9	
    10	| | Partitions | Three routes | Cost to the claim |
    11	|---|---|---|---|
    12	| **F** (fable) | the **physical quantity** | credited bits under the candidate's occupancy; resource energy under the candidate's activation; continuation | **forfeits coordination as a named ablatable route** |
    13	| **A** (astra) | the **information** | local-link innovation; temporal innovation; relational innovation — successive increments of `E[Y \| I0, X_J]` where `Y = B - eta_ref*E` | keeps three named routes; **ablation must propagate through the whole computation**, not zero an output |
    14	
    15	They partition different axes, so they are not variants of one another and **neither is a
    16	special case of the other**.
    17	
    18	## What can and cannot run in parallel
    19	
    20	**Can, and should:**
    21	- Both derive every target from the same coupled-physics quantities — whole-network bits and
    22	  joules, per-user attribution, the three sealed offsets. `EXACTGEN` produces those once and
    23	  both consume them. **The expensive part is shared.**
    24	- Both can be screened **offline, with no training and no policy run**, by computing their
    25	  targets on the same rows and measuring the quantities below.
    26	- The mode-selection change (`R2`/`R3` stale-CQI) sits in the control law, not the route
    27	  layer, and is orthogonal to both.
    28	
    29	**Cannot:**
    30	- A contract v2 declaration names **one** set of three route identities. The choice is
    31	  exclusive **at declaration time**, not at measurement time.
    32	- Full training of both to endpoint would double the panel cost. This screen deliberately
    33	  stops before that.
    34	
    35	## Metrics, fixed now
    36	
    37	Computed for **F**, for **A**, and for the **incumbent** `Psi = Delta F(A) - sum d_i`, on the
    38	same rows, same anchors, same catalogue:
    39	
    40	1. **Pointwise conditioning.** Parts-to-total ratio `(sum |parts|) / |total|` at each
    41	   coalition size, and its growth in size. The incumbent's measured values are 1.57x at pairs
    42	   and 22.99x at the grand row; report every candidate on that same scale. Report the
    43	   **pointwise** figure for all three. A population RMS bound may be reported **alongside**
    44	   but never in place of it, and never compared directly against a pointwise number.
    45	2. **Reachability.** Fraction of anchors at which the third route's term changes the catalogue
    46	   argmax, computed **exactly** (with the exact term, not a learned head), plus the share of
    47	   catalogue rows the term can never reorder.
    48	3. **Learnability of each route, separately.** Held-out `R^2`, within-group pairwise ordering,
    49	   and top-1 argmax agreement, under leave-one-anchor-out and leave-one-world-out, against a
    50	   linear model, a constant predictor, and a high-capacity model on the same inputs. A
    51	   high-capacity failure means the ceiling is **informational**, not architectural.
    52	4. **Distinctness.** Pairwise correlation between the three routes' targets, and the `R^2` of
    53	   each route regressed on the other two. A route that is a function of the others is not a
    54	   separate source, whatever it is named.
    55	5. **Cost.** Wall time to produce each route's target per anchor, so the panel can be costed
    56	   before it is funded.
    57	
    58	## Reading rule, fixed now
    59	
    60	- **No winner is declared on conditioning alone.** Conditioning is necessary, not sufficient:
    61	  a perfectly conditioned decomposition whose routes are not separately learnable, or not
    62	  distinct, fails.
    63	- **A candidate that fails (2) — its third route cannot change the argmax — is rejected for
    64	  the three-route claim**, regardless of how well it scores elsewhere. It may still be
    65	  reported as a better total-score predictor.
    66	- **If both pass every metric**, the choice is **the owner's**, because the deciding question
    67	  is what the paper claims — whether "coordination" survives as a named ablatable route — and
    68	  that is not a measurement.
    69	- **If both fail**, that is reported plainly, with the incumbent's numbers beside them, and
    70	  no third candidate is invented to rescue the outcome in the same breath.
    71	- Nothing here is an EE claim. None of these metrics is pooled EE, and none may be reported
    72	  as one. Committed pooled EE is measured only on a declared panel, later, if one is funded.
    73	
    74	## What this pre-declaration is not
    75	
    76	It does not authorise contract v2, a corpus substitution, a panel, or any training. It fixes
    77	how two proposals will be compared, before either has a number attached.

=================== V025-CONTROLLER-PREDECLARATION-TRAINING-GATE-2026-09-10.md
     1	# Pre-declaration — the gate that must pass before any training run
     2	
     3	**2026-09-10, written BEFORE `FACTORIAL` and `C1REAL` report.** No sealed artefact, constant,
     4	threshold, sign, seed, horizon, price, guard or acceptance rule is changed. This fixes the
     5	condition for starting training while the deciding numbers do not yet exist.
     6	
     7	## The wrong gate, and why I nearly used it
     8	
     9	Earlier today I framed the training gate as: the corpus is real, and the sealed checkpoint
    10	format can express the protocol. Both matter, but **neither is the gate.** The format
    11	question is "can we write it down"; it says nothing about whether writing it down is worth
    12	anything.
    13	
    14	**The gate is the exact ceiling.** Every route's exact marginal is an upper bound on what a
    15	learned head predicting that route can deliver: the exact term hands the selector the true
    16	value, a head hands it an estimate. A route worth `x%` with exact terms is worth **at most**
    17	`x%` with a head, and strictly less whenever the head errs.
    18	
    19	Training before the exact marginals are known would measure a shortfall against an unknown
    20	bound. That is the failure mode recorded earlier today — building a component before building
    21	the contrast that gives it meaning.
    22	
    23	## The gate
    24	
    25	Training may be proposed only when **both** hold, and each is reported before the proposal:
    26	
    27	**G1 — the exact marginals justify it.** `FACTORIAL` (dispatched 08:26 UTC) completes the
    28	`2^3` factorial with exact terms on `FULLSPAN`'s catalogue, anchors, guard, tie-break,
    29	provisioning, price and pooling, reporting `C1_ONLY`, `C2_ONLY`, `C3_ONLY`, the three `DROP`
    30	arms, `FULL` and `BASELINE`, demand-capped. Against the owner's current bar — the three
    31	together substantial, one or two contributing little acceptable — G1 passes when the joint
    32	span is substantial **and** the report states plainly which single-route marginals are
    33	positive and which are not. **No numerical pass mark is invented here; the magnitudes are
    34	reported and the bar is the owner's.**
    35	
    36	**G2 — the target is learnable.** `C1REAL` must show the ceiling on the declared target is
    37	**architectural, not informational**. If a high-capacity model on the same 16 inputs also
    38	fails, the limit is in the information, and no architecture, epoch count or seed repairs it.
    39	**That conclusion is reachable without training and would make training pointless.**
    40	
    41	## What happens under each outcome
    42	
    43	- **G1 and G2 both pass** — propose five production arms, 500 epochs, cadence 100, on the
    44	  exact corpus. That protocol is round-trip verified in the sealed format and resume is

    46	  ceiling a learned head recovers.** It is not the claim.
    47	- **G1 passes, G2 fails** — do not train this decomposition. The targets change first; the two
