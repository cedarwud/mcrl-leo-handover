## 1. What was settled by reading the code before anything was counted

**(1) What the deployable 113-dim observation carries.** `runtime/state_encoding.py::encode_state` builds the
live **112**-dim per-user state as four beam-indexed blocks of `C = 28` (satellite-major, beam-minor):
block 1 `access_vector` (raw one-hot incumbent), block 2 `channel_quality` encoded `log1p(max(γ,0))` (the
nominal, previous-step-interference **linear SINR** γ_a), block 3 `beam_offsets` (raw θ radians), block 4
`beam_loads / num_users` (the **ungated pre-admission** previous-step demand n_a for the physical beam behind
that slot). The 13-dim `contract_fields` block is an ablation switch, **off by default** (ruling C-1). The
**113th** feature is `(T − t)/T`, added by `cf_ratio.py::encode_with_time`. Nothing else is in the observation.
`B_w = BEAM_BANDWIDTH_HZ = 500 MHz / 3 = 166.667 MHz` (`env/link_budget.py:21,28`) and `num_users = 100` are
fixed configuration constants, so `r̂(a) = (B_w/(n_a+1))·log2(1+γ_a)` and every deployable rule below are exact
functions of the deployable observation. Details and line numbers: `PROGRESS.md`, Finding 1.

**(2) The `R_min` question, and how it was resolved.** The brief's original C-Q / C-RC required `R_min` to be
an **existing declared constant of this project**. It was verified first-hand at `05aadf1b` that no such
constant exists: `env/service.py:289-301` records that the only minimum-rate consumer, `required_sinr()`
computing `γ_req(U) = 2^(R^m·U/B^w) − 1`, was **deleted** by PATCH P-22 (W-26) because `R^m = 1 Mbit/s` is
**legacy-only** (ruling C-12, `docs/CONTROLLER-RULINGS-2026-08-22.md:180`) and the active contract
"明文排除最低速率反推"; ruling C-2 (same file, 52-54) forbids target-SINR inversion outright and leaves
`BEAM_POWER_MAX_W = 1.65 W` — a **power** feasibility test that already decides `served` — as the only live
ceiling; `env/link_budget.py` has no rate threshold and no ACM/MODCOD table;
`grep -rnoE "[A-Z][A-Z_0-9]*_BPS" --include=*.py src/` returns **zero hits**; and
`git ls-tree -r --name-only HEAD | grep -c physics_v025` returns **0**, so `RATE_TARGET_BPS = 50e6` is on an
unmerged branch, unreachable from `env/step.py`, and is a **power-control setpoint with no delivered-rate

**The controller upheld the finding and refused an absolute `R_min` (Amendment 11 §1)**, adopting the lane's

## 5. The prospective reading rule that was applied (brief §3, quoted verbatim; unchanged by Amendment 11 §3)

> A candidate advances to a Stage-1 canary only if **all five** hold. The numbers reuse existing project
> conventions; none is invented after seeing a result.
>
> 1. **Deployability**: the policy is an exact function of the current deployable observation (algebraic
>    check). If a candidate is not exactly observation-computable, it is classified privileged and must pass
>    the existing clone test at `R_repr ≥ 0.5` (evaluation and calibration) before any canary.
> 2. **Position in (EE, served, p10)**: either **non-dominated** against T0 and the other retained candidates
>    (no other is at least equal on all three with one strict advantage), **or clearly complementary**, defined
>    as `p10 ≥ 1.10 × T0's p10` on the same set with `served ≥ 0.995` and `EE ≥ 0.95 × T0's EE`.
> 3. **Behavioural distinctness from T0**: action disagreement on shared states **≥ 25 %** (the Ruling 2 §4
>    pre-screen number, kept as a diagnostic, not as the definition of a catfish).
> 4. **Positive value-weighted complementarity**: on the disagreement states, the candidate-better fraction
>    **≥ 0.30** and the complementarity ratio `CR = Σ max(Δ, 0) / Σ |min(Δ, 0)| ≥ 0.5`, with `Δ` the unilateral
>    `Δ(bits − η₀·joules)` defined above. A candidate that merely differs, while losing wherever it differs,
>    fails.
> 5. **No QoS collapse**: `served ≥ 0.995`, `p10 ≥ 0.5 ×` the `A m=2dB` p10 on the same set, and a strictly
>    positive minimum served-user rate.
>
> **Ranking and cap**: survivors are ranked by `CR`; if two are within 10 % relative on `CR`, the higher p10
> ranks first. **At most the best two advance.** If zero qualify, the report says zero — no count is
> manufactured.
>
> **T0 decomposition**: the decomposition is *supported* only if each endpoint separately satisfies conditions
> 1–5 against the fused T0. Otherwise the report states that T0 remains one candidate source and the
> decomposition is rejected as a multi-catfish interpretation.

Applied without amendment. Amendment 11 §2 adds one further rule the report must honour: the two C-Q′ variants
**consume one survivor slot, not two**; the deployable local form is primary, the global form advances only if
the local form fails *and* it clones at `R_repr ≥ 0.5`, and if both pass the deployable one is carried forward.

Three notes on how the rule was read, all recorded before the verdicts were formed:

(i) Condition 2's comparison set was taken to be `{T0, C-Q′-local, C-Q′-global, C-RC′, link endpoint,
activation endpoint}` — T0 and the retained candidates — with the legacy rules, RANDOM and MODQN as references
outside it.

(ii) **Condition 3's "shared states" is ambiguous in the brief** and the ambiguity was resolved *against* the
candidates' interest, not in their favour: brief §2 lists "action agreement and disagreement with T0" among the
per-arm rollout measurements, which `cf_dev.dev_rollout` computes on each arm's **own** trajectory, whereas
§3's phrase "shared states" and the whole counterfactual construction of §2 refer to **T0's** trajectory. The
T0-trajectory number was used, because a policy's own trajectory is not a set of states shared with T0. Both
numbers are in the table. **The reading changes no verdict**: the only arm the two readings straddle is C-RC′
(0.2311 shared vs 0.3337 own), and C-RC′ fails condition 4 independently at `CR = 0.4229`.

(iii) Several arms clear condition 2 only through a served-fraction margin of `+4·10⁻⁵` over T0 — one
user-step in 24,000, which is noise. It is reported as such rather than silently banked. It changes no verdict:
every arm that clears condition 2 that way fails condition 3 or 4 outright.

## 6. The three required statements

**(a) Is the T0 decomposition supported or rejected? — REJECTED.**

* **Link endpoint (`c = 0`, `MAX_NOMINAL_GAIN`)**: c1 ✓, c2 ✓ (only by the noise-level served margin),
  **c3 ✗ (0.1982 < 0.25)**, **c4 ✗ (CR 0.3253 < 0.5)**, c5 ✓.
* **Activation endpoint (high `c`, `B1_NO_NEW_BEAM`)**: c1 ✓, **c2 ✗ (dominated by T0 on all three; p10 is
  0.32 × T0's, nowhere near the 1.10 × complementarity route)**, c3 ✓ (0.2567), **c4 ✗ (CR 0.3920 < 0.5)**,
  **c5 ✗ (p10 0.638 × the `A m=2dB` floor)**.

Neither endpoint separately satisfies conditions 1–5 against the fused T0, so by brief §3 the decomposition is
**rejected as a multi-catfish interpretation, and T0 remains one candidate source.** The mechanism is legible:
fused T0 beats *both* of its endpoints on pooled EE (1.1766e8 against 1.1003e8 and 1.0445e8) while holding a
better rate tail than the activation endpoint and better EE than the link endpoint. Neither endpoint owns a
state subset where it beats fused T0 in aggregate — both have `Σ Δ < 0` and `CR < 0.5` on exactly the states
where they disagree. The `c = 1` mixture, not either term, is where the value sits.

**(b) Which zero to two candidates advance? — ZERO.**

All three prospectively specified candidates of Amendment 11 §2 were measured and all three **DROP**:

* **C-Q′-local** fails **condition 3** by two orders of magnitude: 57 differing decisions out of 24,000
  (0.24 %). It is T0 with a rounding error attached — pooled EE within 0.012 % of T0's and an identical served
  fraction. It is the only arm in the lane to clear condition 4 (`CR = 0.5037`, better-fraction 0.4386), which
  is exactly the point: the qualifying set `{r̂_u(a) ≥ q10^u(t)}` almost never excludes the action T0 was going
  to take, so the rule has nothing left to add.
* **C-Q′-global** fails **conditions 3 and 4** (disagreement 0.0760; `CR = 0.4798`). Because it fails on the
  merits, its clone screen at `R_repr ≥ 0.5` **was not run**: condition 1's clone test is a gate on advancement,
  and an arm failing conditions 3 and 4 cannot advance at any `R_repr`. That is recorded as a deliberate
  omission with its reason, not a gap. The one-survivor-slot rule of Amendment 11 §2 is therefore moot.
* **C-RC′** fails **condition 4** (`CR = 0.4229`, better-fraction 0.3149) and also condition 3 on the
  shared-state reading (0.2311; 0.3337 on its own trajectory). It is the only new candidate that is genuinely
  behaviourally different, and it loses value where it differs: `Σ Δ = −1.54e13`, median Δ `−3.09e9`, and
  23.7 % of its deviations are QoS-invalid — the highest share of any arm measured.

**Amendment 11 §4's escape hatch was not needed and is not being invoked**: no candidate required a number
that is not already in the project. The re-specification succeeded as a specification; the candidates failed as
hypotheses. `q10` is a same-step order statistic of `r̂` and 10 % is the project's existing tail convention, so
this Stage 0 has consumed no new constant of any kind.

Every remaining rolled policy is a declared control, and each fails at least one of conditions 2–5, so no
promotion could produce a survivor either. **Zero prospectively specified new candidates advance, alongside the
completed controls, the T0 decomposition endpoints and the complementarity diagnostic. No count was
manufactured, and the ranking and two-survivor cap are moot.**

Flagged for the controller, not acted on: `A m=12dB` — a legacy control, explicitly "not a hypothesis" in
brief §1 — is the only rolled policy that clears four of the five conditions (deployable ✓, non-dominated with
p10 at 1.16 × T0 ✓, disagreement 0.5286 ✓, no QoS collapse ✓) and misses only condition 4, by 3 % relative
(`CR = 0.4847` against the 0.50 bar), with a candidate-better fraction of 0.3852. Reclassifying a declared
control as a candidate is a scope change and the controller's call, not this lane's; it is reported because
suppressing a four-of-five near-miss would be the more misleading choice.

A methodological note the controller may want before any successor lane is opened: across nine measured arms
spanning the gain axis, the load axis, two hysteresis depths, both tail-restricted forms and both order-statistic
scopes, **nothing cleared both gates**, and every arm lost value in aggregate exactly where it differed from T0.
The plausible reading is not that the candidate list was unlucky but that the single-step unilateral margin
around T0 is genuinely thin in this physics — in which case a second source, if one exists, is more likely to
come from information T0 does not have (multi-step or cross-user structure) than from another reweighting of
the same two observation blocks. That is a hypothesis this lane did not test and is not authorised to test.
