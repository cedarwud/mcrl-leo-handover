## Binding review conclusions

1. The removal-counterfactual marginal reward is a valid C3 difference reward
   for one focal deviation with all other actions fixed. The unilateral power
   identity and strict bottleneck power certificate pass.
2. It is not yet authorised as Main's R3 reward: its population sum is not
   `-P_system`, non-focal rewards change on unchanged actions, and Main's 112-D
   observation omits the exact bottleneck variables. Treat it as C3-private
   credit until aliasing and replay-credit tests pass.
3. The old median-development decision `REJECT_MEDIAN_RATE_RULE` is permanent
   under that protocol. The same fixed rule may be registered as a **new
   hypothesis** on disjoint seeds; the old result cannot be relabelled.
4. The new shadow primary is EE with the `phi1` **time** cost included. Until
   `E_HO` closes, no handover-energy value may be assumed. Throughput is
   descriptive only; no non-inferiority margin or claim is allowed.
5. For each event, report the energy break-even quantity from cumulative saved
   power so `E_HO` can later be compared without rerunning the campaign.
6. Disclose multiplicity: the median rule was the third guard examined on the
   development pool and selected exactly the five immediate-EE-positive rows.
7. Hidden-state aliasing is testable rather than automatically fatal. Log each
   Main 112-D observation with source-qualified, power-certified,
   guard-retained, realised EE-sign, and marginal-reward labels. Check
   tolerance-level observation collisions and 1-nearest-neighbour label
   consistency against a permutation baseline.
