# C1/C2 audit — 2026-09-09

`DIAGNOSTIC_NOT_CLAIM`

## Executive finding

**The most consequential defect is that the production pilot corpus does not train C1 and C2 on their contracted exact targets.** [READ] The enabled source path is explicitly a proxy (`PILOT_PRIMITIVE_SOURCE_FALLBACK = True`), so C1 is labelled from a bounded log link-gain ratio and C2 from three such ratios (or an outage constant), while off-axis alternatives are assigned zero. The exact evaluator path immediately below it is dead under that flag. (`scripts/run_v025_pilot_c3.py:89`, `scripts/run_v025_pilot_c3.py:403-445`, `scripts/run_v025_pilot_c3.py:460-462`, `scripts/run_v025_pilot_c3.py:525-543`, `scripts/run_v025_pilot_c3.py:544-611`, `scripts/run_v025_pilot_c3.py:674-676`)

[READ] This is not a harmless approximation to the sealed obligation: the contract defines C1 from the whole-network objective increment, including energy and the signed preference term, and C2 as a separate three-offset continuation value. (`V025-STAGES-6-8-CONTRACT-v1-2026-09-08.md:11-16`; `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:130-173`, `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:251-310`) [READ] The pilot report acknowledges that exact full-network C1/C2 labels were considered too expensive and that approximate primitive labels were used, including zero off-axis labels and boundary/fallback conventions. (`V025-PILOT-MIN-REPORT-2026-09-09.md:125-134`)

[EXEC] On the ten saved size-100 decisions (five anchors × two learner seeds), a bounded replay through the current exact evaluator found mean exact C1 `+34.990`, exact C2 `-151.063`, and exact additive total `-116.073`; the learned means were `+15.962`, `+90.539`, and `+106.501`. C2 therefore had a one-sided upward error of `+241.602` on average and the learned total missed the exact C1/C2 cancellation by `+222.573`; eight of ten total signs were wrong. Inputs were the saved decisions in `artifacts/v025-pilot-min-20260909-PILOT_NOT_CLAIM/evaluation/PILOT_NOT_CLAIM-raw-receipts.jsonl:1`; the replay used the production target definitions at `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:130-173` and `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:251-310` and the production encoders at `src/mcrl/stagec_v025/state.py:396-481`.

[INFERENCE] The passing C1/C2 gates therefore establish wiring and some algebraic invariants, not that the learned singleton/additive path estimates the physical quantities it is named for. The observed size-100 failure is principally consistent with target substitution and feature insufficiency, not merely an underfit regression.

## 3. Feature sufficiency

### Encoder audit

[READ] Q1 is a 16-scalar vector and Q2 is a 22-scalar vector. (`src/mcrl/stagec_v025/state.py:29-46`, `src/mcrl/stagec_v025/state.py:48-86`) [READ] The encoders retain focal-action summaries, occupancy/activity summaries, history flags, and a few offset summaries, then normalize them. (`src/mcrl/stagec_v025/state.py:396-481`) [READ] The learner routes only those vectors to the C1/C2 heads; it does not pass action identity, the background association graph, per-interferer cross-gains, a coupled-power solution, or full projected `NetworkOutcome` objects to either head. (`src/mcrl/stagec_v025/learner.py:167-172`, `src/mcrl/stagec_v025/learner.py:187-260`)

[READ] This is the same class of compression defect found in C3: a structured whole-network quantity is reduced to aggregates before the head sees it. The exact C1 answer depends on whole-network bits, joules, and preference, while exact C2 depends on complete future projected outcomes. (`src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:130-209`, `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:251-310`) [INFERENCE] No deterministic function of Q1/Q2 alone can be exact over all physically admissible states if those hidden network outcomes can vary while the vectors stay fixed.

### Constructed collision

[EXEC] I constructed two distinct physical actions, `(satellite_id=1, beam_id=1)` and `(satellite_id=2, beam_id=2)`, with identical legal focal summaries and identical Q1/Q2 forecast summaries. In state A, production target helpers returned exact C1 `+100` and C2 `+300`; in state B they returned exact C1 `-10` and C2 `-30`. Production `extract_source_rows` mapped both states to byte-for-byte identical Q1 vectors and identical Q2 vectors. (`src/mcrl/stagec_v025/state.py:396-481`; target computation at `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:154-173`, `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:251-310`; physical action/outcome structures at `src/mcrl/physics_v025/targets.py:176-209`)

[INFERENCE] This is a decisive interface-level collision: physically distinct states with different correct signs can be indistinguishable to both heads. [INFERENCE] The fixture uses valid synthetic `NetworkOutcome` objects; it proves insufficiency of the declared function interface, but it does not claim that the current V025 simulator naturally emits this exact numeric twin.

[EXEC] In the 176,223 production rows, 938 exact Q1-vector keys and 100 exact Q2-vector keys were shared by multiple physical action identities; no exact-vector key had differing proxy labels. (`artifacts/v025-pilot-c3-20260909-PILOT_NOT_CLAIM/rows/world-1/PILOT_NOT_CLAIM-source-anchor-000.jsonl:2`; all sibling source shards named by `artifacts/v025-pilot-c3-20260909-PILOT_NOT_CLAIM/PILOT_NOT_CLAIM-rows-manifest.json:1`) [INFERENCE] The absence of differing labels is expected, not exculpatory: the fallback labels are themselves functions of the compressed/local primitives, so the corpus cannot expose distinctions its label generator discarded.
