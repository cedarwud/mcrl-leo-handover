**Prospective reading rules for the five pending measurements.**

These rules interpret results; they do not authorize runs or alter the existing experiment. If a job has already completed, the rules remain prospective to my reading because I have not opened its output. A validity failure should be reported as such, preserving the original result; it is not permission to hunt for a favourable rerun.

**OBJMISMATCH.** A legal single-user move improving capped committed EE disproves the relevant endpoint's local optimality **for that objective and neighbourhood**. It does not invalidate its old fixed-price certificate. Count all tested endpoints, the number with improving moves, and the attainable reported gain, with bits, joules and guard semantics. A high flip rate with negligible gain means mismatch without demonstrated practical importance. A small number of large gains means concentrated opportunity, not universal failure.

A complete negative census establishes local optimality only on the tested legal singleton neighbourhood. A sampled negative result means “none found.” Neither excludes other starts, swaps or coordinated controls. Also distinguish per-anchor ratio optimization from whole-panel ratio optimization. With positive energy, a candidate improves the panel when `ΔN−eta_panel*ΔE>0`, where `eta_panel` is the current pooled ratio. Testing against each anchor's own eta answers a different question.
