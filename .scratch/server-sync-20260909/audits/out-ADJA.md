**Derived, conditional planning arithmetic.** For a paired log-contrast with crossed random effects,

`L_ds = μ + a_d + b_s + e_ds`,

`Var(mean L) = σ_date²/D + σ_seed²/S + σ_residual²/(D S)`.

Increasing seeds reduces the latter two terms; it cannot remove `σ_date²/D`. At D=1 the between-date component cannot be estimated from that panel. A date bootstrap repeatedly resamples the same date, so any resulting interval cannot support generalization across dates. That is the sound part of the wall. “More seeds cannot help at fixed D” is too strong; “more seeds cannot repair missing date replication” is correct.

Using the planning values σ_date=0.05, σ_seed=0.01, zero residual variance and an optimistic D=166 gives SE=0.004616 at 16 seeds and a limiting SE=0.003881. A Gaussian lower-bound approximation gives

`power ≈ Φ((log(1+Δ) − log(1.005))/SE − 1.959964)`.

| Assumed true relative effect | 16 seeds | Infinite-seed limit |
|---|---:|---:|
| +0.899421% | 13.6% | 17.4% |
| +1.000000% | 18.8% | 24.8% |
| +1.290000% | 39.6% | 52.3% |

These are arithmetic illustrations, **not measured power of the percentile bootstrap**. They express low power, not zero probability of detection. For a single +1% contrast, about 797 independent dates would be needed for 80% power even with infinitely many seeds under this approximation. Against a zero margin instead, the infinite-seed +1% power would be about 72.7%; silently switching between positivity and the sealed +0.5% practical margin materially changes the argument.

The approximation must not be promoted into the project's power result. Its variance must describe the **paired contrast**, not the raw EE spread of individual arms; common date shocks can cancel. For unequal-energy pooled ratios, the relevant variance is that of the pooled estimator's influence contributions, not automatically an unweighted average of datewise log ratios. The inspected merger correctly pools additive totals and applies the same product of date and seed resampling weights to both arms (`R/src/mcrl/stagec_v025/merge.py:178–183,272–343`). Separate worlds or many anchors on one date do not become independent dates. Temporal independence across archive dates also requires justification.
