**A fourth option is a conditional benchmark with independent world draws.** Fix the archive A, date weights w_d and target distribution of within-date randomisations Z. Freeze the trained policies, preprocessing, calibration, checkpoint choice and all analysis rules. Generate evaluation worlds independently of the source corpus conditional on A and date, with fresh random streams and auditable world/trajectory lineage. State the claim as performance on fresh scenarios from this fixed archive’s familiar-date distribution.

For an additive paired outcome g and m_d fresh worlds per date, conditional independence gives

\[
\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_d w_d\frac{1}{m_d}\sum_i g(\pi,A,d,Z_{di})\mid A,\text{training}\right]
=\sum_d w_d\,\mathbb E_Z[g(\pi,A,d,Z)].
\]

Thus date overlap does not bias that conditional performance estimate, even if the trained policy specialises to those dates. This is a proof under an explicit sampling model, not an assertion that the current implementation satisfies it. Distinct seed strings alone are insufficient; the generated dependencies must match the independence claim. For pooled EE, the argument applies to estimating bits and energy totals; their finite-sample ratio is not generally unbiased. Neither this design nor the equation supports unseen-date or future-constellation generalisation.
