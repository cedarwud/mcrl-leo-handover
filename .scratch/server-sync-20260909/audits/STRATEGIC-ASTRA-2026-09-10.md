The controller has treated today’s discoveries as reasons to repair the original research programme. They are stronger reasons to replace its primary question. It does not establish that three learned components are useful, much less that each clears a prescribed marginal-benefit threshold.

**2. Make the mechanism the contribution.** The defensible question is: *When do occupancy-dependent power control, mode selection and beam activation create profitable coordinated association changes that a specified unilateral improvement procedure misses?*

There is a concrete answer. In the occupancy witness, increasing occupancy raises the nominal rate-target mode and RF power. After conservative mode selection, occupancies one and two support no transmitted mode; occupancy three does. The delivered-bit interaction exists with zero interference. In the swap witness, either individual move breaks service on its source beam, while the joint swap preserves service and saves energy. These are useful explanations of coordinated action, demonstrated through the actual engine. They are stronger scientific objects than a neural head’s name. The oracle result is not a mathematical ceiling. Its comparator is a local fixed point reached by one specified unilateral procedure, under its objective and constraints. This mechanism deserves the remaining effort because its decisive uncertainty is identifiable: whether the modelling and control assumptions make it relevant beyond these examples. The learned-controller programme still has unresolved targets, representation, decision quality, timing and independent evaluation. **1. Retire the three-component conjunction as the primary design.** Its statistical formulation can be coherent while its scientific purpose is wrong.

The deeper alternative is to ask whether the occupancy-induced blackout should exist in the first place. Consider a service-aware power/mode policy that can allocate enough power for a feasible transmitted mode without requiring two additional users to arrive. Compare that policy with the existing rate-target/back-off law under the same physical constraints and explicit service obligations. If much of the coordination gain disappears, the contribution becomes a diagnosis of the control law and its remedy. That could be more useful than teaching three networks to compensate for it. I have not seen that comparison in the supplied record.

Three changes of viewpoint follow:

- **Decision unit:** choose beam occupancy patterns and complete user bundles, then resolve individual assignments. The physical mechanism suggests these units directly. A deterministic occupancy-aware coalition proposer with exact validation is the comparator a learned coordinator should have to beat.
- **Objective:** examine minimum energy for an explicitly specified delivered-service obligation, alongside the existing pooled-EE ratio. This would reveal whether the gain represents cheaper adequate service or movement along a throughput–energy tradeoff. The 50 Mbit/s power-control setpoint must not silently become a demonstrated service guarantee.
- **Operational claim:** measure useful validated improvement available by ten seconds against an anytime unilateral method and the simple mechanism-aware proposer. Count fallback outcomes and all decision work. If learning eventually wins, ask whether it reduces expensive evaluations or finds better candidates sooner. Predicting an exact score after paying to evaluate every candidate provides no automatic acceleration benefit.

If a learned contribution is mandatory, first consider learning to accelerate the expensive unilateral search itself. That targets the largest demonstrated improvement over the carrier and the clearest operational bottleneck. The existing figures do not establish that learning can recover that gain cheaply; they identify a more consequential approximation problem than insisting that every route earns its own half percent.

My next allocation would be to write the conditional mechanism argument and resolve its central modelling objection. I would permit a learning continuation only after naming an outcome-based advantage over an appropriate simple controller that the continuation is designed to establish. Another upward segment of prediction accuracy would not meet that condition.
