# V025 — controller decisions on the stage-C cost rehearsal (2026-09-09 ≈ 02:35 UTC; before any successor training)

Source: `V025-STAGEC-COST-REHEARSAL-2026-09-09.md` (Opus 5, synthetic fixtures). Measured: training is exactly linear in batch rows (5.195e-5 s per pair per epoch); merge and source generation are negligible; peak RSS ≈ 270 MiB per lineage; the fork-per-step evaluation cost (0.68 core-h) buys the cancellation guarantee and is not optimised away.

1. **Adopted immediately (engineering, contract-neutral):** (a) hoist the constant neutral batches out of the epoch loop (`learner.py:1027,:1034` rebuild and re-hash a deterministic zeroed-target batch every epoch) — measured 1.91× on training; (b) raise training concurrency from 4 to 16 lineages (independent, single-threaded numpy, ≈ 4.3 GB total) — 4× wall reduction at zero compute cost, and 16 is the ceiling worth committing.
2. **Not adopted: capping the sealed aggregate batch (lever 2).** It changes the learner's data and therefore the experiment. It stays available as a **pre-declared** option to be sealed *before* source generation if the re-projection below shows the training wall time exceeding 12 h after items 1(a) and 1(b); the reduction, if taken, is a stratified 4× with the strata declared in the same seal.
3. **Scale correction required.** The projection assumed 20 users per step × 10 actions per user; the sealed panel is 100 users with a top-8 shortlist, i.e. ≈ 4× more rows, and training is linear in rows. The realistic training figure is therefore ≈ 4× the reported 591 core-h before lever 1(a), and the numbers in the rehearsal must not be quoted as the plan. A re-projection at the sealed scale is commissioned now.
4. **The decisive planning number is the coordinator's mean solve time, not the 10 s deadline.** At 1 s mean, evaluation is ≈ 299 core-h (14.9 h at 20 cores); at the 10 s cancellation bound it is 6.2 days. The stage-4g real-anchor measurement therefore reports the **mean and the p95** per decision, not only the worst case, and the evaluation projection is recomputed from the measured mean.
5. Every number here is an engineering measurement on synthetic fixtures; none of it enters any scientific claim, and none of it may change a sealed scientific rule.
