## 2. Diagnosis: why a learnable, held‑out‑predictive signal did not convert

**First‑order physics makes the current‑slot closure event EE‑neutral.** From `link_budget.py`/`step.py`: per‑user rate is (B/U)·log₂(1+γ), so a beam's sum‑rate is B·mean SE, load‑invariant; beam energy is p/ξ(p)+0.338 W with p = max served link power, i.e. 6.3–8.7 W per active beam regardless of occupancy. On the F1 world: 367 W system power ≈ 42–58 active beams for 100 users ≈ 2 users/beam, 435 Mb/s per user, SE ≈ 5.2. Closing a two‑user beam removes ≈2% of energy and ≈2% of bits; η moves only through second‑order terms (source‑beam SE/P relative to the system average, PA √‑curvature, interference). R7's −0.049% is this cancellation measured. The energy lever is real but it is not a *bits* externality, and C3's ownership statement bars energy.

**Factoredness vs learnability (Wolpert–Tumer; Agogino–Tumer).** The difference reward D_u = G(b[u←a]) − G(b) is factored: for a *unilateral* change, sign(ΔD_u) = sign(ΔG). Nothing deployed was D_u. PNFE/MONE added the non‑focal *part* of D_u to Q1+O2; CSE/EC replaced marginal cost with average‑cost shares, which are not factored — read the declared formula: a user opening a beam alone has Δshare − ΔE = −share_u(b), so z_F = +λ·share_u(b) > 0 and z_D adds the positive load‑relief of leaving; both reward abandoning shared beams. F1's outcome (D: bits +6.9%, energy +8.4%; F: bits +14.8%, energy +20.2%) is the formula executing. LC‑SRS is efficient (Σ(own_i+z3_i) = G(11)−G(00)) but z3_i = nonfocal_i + Ψ/2 pays each member half the interaction term whether or not the partner moves. That is exactly the harmful‑partial signature R7 recorded (`harmful_partial` FAIL, topology consistency 0.536): a Shapley *allocation* is not an *incentive* under independent execution. Factoredness also guarantees nothing about additivity: Σ_u D_u ≠ ΔG for joint moves, and here the interaction terms are first‑order (load 1/U coupling, max‑power coupling, beam activation). V0.10 measured it — surplus sums positive in 60/60 diagnostics, realised joint direction reversed in 28.

**Synchronous best response has no monotonicity.** Exact difference‑reward targets define an exact potential game with potential G; asynchronous best response converges to a unilateral local optimum, synchronous best response can oscillate (the beam‑activation expansion of V0.10/V0.11 is the textbook congestion oscillation). The deployment rule is synchronous, once, from a background b that 60–100% of users leave (V0.9 handover rates), so the "others hold" counterfactual every target is computed at is false at execution.

**Additive composition has a measurable noise floor that dwarfs the prize.** Q1+Q2 margins are 0.14–0.19κ (V0.14), Q2's own centring error 0.09–0.11κ, exact z3 for teacher changes 0.47κ. So the exact term dominates when active and learner off‑support error flips decisions at the same scale as the margins. κ itself (1.01e10 bits ≈ 0.77 of one user‑step's bits) is a correct unit; the defect is that the rule has no error‑aware element. A global rescale is forbidden and would shrink the teacher's reach too.

**Label price ≠ scored ratio.** λ is frozen at 1.184e8 bits/J; R7 world η is 122.3–129.8e6, so η/λ = 1.03–1.10: labels under‑charge energy by 3–10% on those worlds. For a beam‑opening move (λΔE ≈ 2.5κ) that is 0.08–0.25κ of label bias — decision‑relevant — and the events the head must rank sit at |surplus| ≈ 0.

**Fixed‑policy evaluation.** The heads are supervised regressors deployed one‑shot; the COIN guarantees are about adaptation dynamics, which never run here. C1 and C2 are functions of the focal user's own choice given a state, so this costs them little; C3 is the only head whose estimand is a function of the same‑slot joint action, and the deployment has no channel for same‑slot information. Side finding to verify: `marginal_network_power_surface` (`ee_axis_ops3.py:478–523`) computes P^N(background+focal) − P^N(background) with the background still containing the focal's own beam — joins are charged, vacating an origin beam is never credited. If the C1 opening surplus (`ee_axis_opening_source.py`, not shipped) does the same, no head in the frozen partition prices origin relief, and the entire consolidation incentive was delegated to a head barred from carrying energy.
