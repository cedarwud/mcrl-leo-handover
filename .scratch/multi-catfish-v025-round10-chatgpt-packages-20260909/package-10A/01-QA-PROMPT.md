# Round 10A — Is this still multi-agent reinforcement learning, and is the learner sound?
You are a machine-learning methods referee. The attached contract and spec describe the learning stage of a LEO satellite handover/association study whose thesis contribution is framed as multi-agent reinforcement learning with three value components (C1 immediate energy-efficiency surplus, C2 forecast/persistence, C3 coordination). As currently specified, the learner is: **pairwise zero-bootstrap regression** of exact oracle targets onto per-user, per-action feature rows, trained on deterministic aggregate batches for a fixed number of epochs, with no next-state term and no environment interaction during training; deployment is a per-user masked argmax of the summed normalised heads, plus a set-level coordinator that evaluates complete configurations with an exact nominal physics model.
1. Is this reinforcement learning in any defensible sense? If it is supervised regression onto oracle labels (behaviour-cloning of a model-based optimiser), say so plainly and state what the paper may and may not claim. What is lost by having no bootstrapping, no exploration and no policy improvement loop?
2. The targets are computed by an exact model that is also available at deployment. What, then, is the learned component actually for? Enumerate the defensible reasons (computation, generalisation to unseen states, robustness to model error, representation) and the evidence each requires.
3. Generalisation: training and evaluation worlds are drawn from the same simulator with different seeds and TLE dates; there is no held-out test split by owner decision. What can be claimed about generalisation, and what is the strongest defensible statement given only date-clustered internal panels?
4. Is the per-user argmax of a sum of two independently regressed heads a sound decision rule? What pathologies should be tested for (scale mismatch, non-commensurate targets, argmax instability, distribution shift between the oracle's states and the policy's own trajectory)?
5. If a referee says "this is a supervised surrogate of an optimiser, not MARL", what is the minimal honest reframing that preserves a real contribution? Give two alternative framings with their required evidence.
