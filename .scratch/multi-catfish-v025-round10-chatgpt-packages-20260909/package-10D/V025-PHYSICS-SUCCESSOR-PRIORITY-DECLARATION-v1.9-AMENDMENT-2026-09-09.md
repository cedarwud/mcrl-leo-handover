# V025 priority declaration — v1.9 amendment (controller; sealed 2026-09-09 ≈ 02:20 UTC, before any formal successor outcome)

v1.0–v1.8 are preserved. This amendment corrects two defects in v1.6 §1 and one inconsistency in v1.6 §4 found by the cross-model adjudication `ADJUDICATION-V16-PREREGISTRATION-CODEX-GPT6-ASTRA-2026-09-09.md` (`PREREGISTRATION=INTACT | MARGIN_RULE=REVISE | TRICHOTOMY=REVISE`). The controller's earlier claim that v1.6 was "uniformly stricter" is withdrawn.

1. **The margin must not be re-solved into transmit power (defect).** Under v1.6 as written, scoring recomputed the required power from the attenuated gain, giving p_m = p_N/q, so the link again targeted exactly Γ: the margin bought no reserve, only more power and more interference, and it changed feasibility at the cap. Corrected construction: **executed and predicted transmit power are computed from the nominal gains** (unchanged); the quantile enters **only the predicted reception of the wanted link** used to decide the transmitted mode and predicted service. Formally, the selection view uses p = p_N(ĥ_nominal) and predicts SINR_pred = q·ĥ_nominal·p / (N₀W + Î), so a link is predicted to decode only if it clears its mode threshold with the reserve q.
2. **The quantile applies to the wanted link only (defect).** Applying the same q to interfering links leaves the signal-to-interference ratio unchanged and only scales noise (SINR = S/(N/q + I)), so a uniform application is not conservative and distorts interference-limited cases. Interference terms keep their nominal values in the selection view.
3. **The quantile is of the complete fading product.** The declared channel is G = R · 10^(−(X + L_c(e))/10) with Rician power fading R, Gaussian shadow loss X and deterministic scintillation L_c(e); v1.6's "shadow + scintillation" wording is corrected. The α-quantile is computed by the sealed procedure: 200 000 draws of the declared product at the link's actual elevation with a fixed derivation seed from the domain rule, cached per (elevation bin of 0.5°, α); the KAT checks the cached quantile against a direct evaluation to 1e-3 relative.
4. **Per-arm ranking keys are explicit (defect).** Using the full F_m for every arm would restore the removed contribution. The immediate ranking key is the arm's own score: FULL → C1_m + C3_m (secondary C2); DROP_C1 → C3_m (secondary C2); DROP_C2 → C1_m + C3_m (secondary: a fixed deterministic rule); DROP_C3 → C1_m (secondary C2). Top-M pruning, any feasibility guard and any tie-break use the same arm-specific key; no arm may rank, prune or reject using a score it has had removed.
5. **C2's oracle effect is expected to be zero by construction, and that is an admissible result.** With a unique immediate maximiser, a tie-break-only continuation cannot change the selected profile, so FULL and DROP_C2 coincide. The engine reports the tie frequency; a zero set-level C2 marginal is reported as such and never patched by substituting Q2's separate effect on the proposal. **C2's certificate in the physics matrix is therefore forecast validity, not a selection marginal**: the three-offset continuation's predicted change is compared with the realised change of the same profiles (per offset, integration error and fading error separated, per v1.6 §2). C2's decision value is tested in the learned stage, where Q2 shapes the proposal.
6. **Admission trichotomy restated so it is monotone (v1.6 §4 superseded).** `ADMIT_FULL`: all load-bearing certificates pass — S0 realised gain over BASE ≥ +1 %, S0 > certified S_UNI beyond the certified numerical error, C1's oracle marginal positive beyond numerical error, C3's interaction term decision-relevant (the coordinator's choice differs from the additive selection at a reported non-trivial fraction of anchors and the realised contrast is positive), C2's forecast validity established, QoS/validity/deadline pass. `ADMIT_C1C2`: identical except that the C3 conditions are not met; training is admitted, C3 is carried as an evaluated layer, and the resulting learned C3 statement is explicitly labelled as lacking an oracle certificate and is therefore a weaker claim than under `ADMIT_FULL`. `NOT_ADMITTED`: C1's oracle marginal or the QoS/validity/deadline conditions fail → contingency ladder rung 1. No branch weakens a condition another branch imposes on the same factor.
7. **Prospectively frozen robustness diagnostic (astra's decisive test).** Before the formal matrix, a paired closed-loop quantile sweep over α ∈ {0.05, 0.10, 0.25} plus the unchanged nominal selector, on common exogenous worlds, each arm keeping its own trajectory and deadline/fallback accounting, evaluated at 48 boundaries. It is a robustness report, never a selector of the primary α; α = 0.10 stays primary regardless of the sweep's outcome. The top-1 agreement diagnostic of v1.6 is retained but is explicitly insufficient on its own.
8. **Required conformance tests carried from the adjudication:** seal-and-panel separation audit; the quantile sweep of item 7; power/interference/48-boundary invariance KATs (executed power identical with and without the margin; interference unchanged; committed-profile endpoint bit-identical); ablation-and-label conformance including the unique-maximiser C2 case; an admission truth table over certificate states; conjunction and QoS null calibration.
