## Amendments
1. **Own sensitivity cells for the primary.** The rate-target primary (a-r) and its FDM sibling (a′-r) receive their own treatment cells so that robustness is not borrowed from a-γ: add `a-rS`, `a-rH`, `a-rSH`, `a-rT` and `a′-rS`, `a′-rH`, `a′-rSH`, `a′-rT` (treatments as defined in astra round 3 §4). Priority order becomes: `a-r0 > a′-r0 > a-γ0 > b0 > a′-γ0`, then S in the order `a-r, a′-r, a-γ, b, a′-γ`, then H, then SH, then T in the same architecture order; U cells remain diagnostic-only. Total: 28 primary-eligible cells + diagnostic U cells.
2. **U split.** The uncapped-Shannon diagnostic is split into `U-cap` (SE cap removed, margin kept) and `U-margin` (cap kept, margin removed) for each of the three original architectures (6 diagnostic cells) so the two effects are separable. Diagnostic only; never primary-eligible.
3. **Uncertainty reporting.** The primary uncertainty statement for every contrast is a **cluster bootstrap that recomputes ΣB/ΣE per resample** (clusters = TLE dates × training seeds as declared in the evaluation design); paired per-world log-EE intervals are supplementary and are never substituted for the pooled-ratio interval.
4. **Claim wording.** The success claim is conditional and intersection–union: "with the other two components enabled, each of the three FULL − DROP pooled-EE contrasts exceeds its prespecified margin δ = +0.5 pp with the 95 % lower bound above it, and FULL is QoS non-inferior to each DROP"; the all-neutral cell supports the FULL − ALL_NEUTRAL comparison; main effects across all backgrounds are not claimed without the full 2³ factorial.
5. **Φ semantics.** Because interruption (treatment H) already removes useful time, Φ₁/Φ₂ are declared to price *signalling/QoS preference* (not time, not joules); they are reported alongside the handover rate as co-primary QoS outcomes, and the reward/coordinator use the same event ledger and coefficients.
6. **Treatment T** is the left-endpoint legacy snapshot (controller decision item 3) and is reported against the converged integral at the same decision instant.
7. **Provenance labels** (from the Deep Research): γ*, r*, P_b/cap, W/α, SE_max, margin, SINR_min, OBO/p_sat, η_max/PA curve, standby (primary and sensitivity), circuit/baseband, bus exclusion, handover event energy, blackout mapping, Δt/quadrature, D2-derived gates and N = 4 refresh are all carried as **declared benchmark constants** (`VERIFY_SOURCE` where a physical claim would be implied); the paper's system-model section states this once. N = 4 is named a candidate-refresh period, not a dwell.
