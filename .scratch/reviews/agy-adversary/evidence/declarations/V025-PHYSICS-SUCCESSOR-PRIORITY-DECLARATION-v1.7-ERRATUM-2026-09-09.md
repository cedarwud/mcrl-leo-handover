# V025 priority declaration — v1.7 erratum (controller; sealed 2026-09-09 ≈ 02:10 UTC, before any formal successor outcome)

v1.0–v1.6 are preserved unchanged. This erratum corrects wording and resolves precedence conflicts found by the round-9A outside review. No constant, arm, margin, panel or decision rule changes.

1. **The margin rationale is renamed, not re-justified.** v1.6 §1 called the q₁₀ level "the standard 90 %-availability link-budget convention". That is withdrawn: a per-link 10th-percentile channel-gain quantile does not establish 90 % network availability, and no such universal convention is claimed. The rule is named exactly what it is: a **pre-specified tenth-percentile channel-gain scoring rule**, applied identically to every arm, with the level fixed before any formal outcome and disclosed as having followed development inspection. Its diagnostic sensitivity at q₀₅/q₂₅ stands.
2. **Two decompositions, never mixed (resolves the target-consistency ambiguity raised by round 9D).** (i) The **selection-time decomposition** uses F_m (margin-adjusted, 5-boundary/decision-instant selection view): dᵢ^m, Ψ_A^m, V_CA and the reversal frequency are all computed from F_m and are the mechanism statistics. (ii) The **outcome decomposition** uses the realised 48-boundary endpoint F: C1, C2, C3 labels, the credit split and every certificate reported against the endpoint are computed from F. A statistic never mixes the two, and each reported quantity names which F it came from.
3. **Interaction labels are not capped; only the per-user split is.** Computing Ψ_A needs the baseline, the |A| singletons and one joint evaluation, i.e. |A| + 2 evaluations. Ψ_A is therefore computed for **every selected coalition regardless of size**; only the exact per-user Shapley attribution stays capped at |A| ≤ 4 (reporting only), with `credit_split = NOT_COMPUTED_LARGE_SET` above it. This restates the 4c-gate decision item 1 and overrides any narrower reading of contract v1 §B5.
4. **Precedence between v1.5 §1 and the contingency ladder Rung 1.** v1.5 §1 governs the **primary claim**: only `a-r0` can produce the paper's primary result, and no sensitivity or regime cell can change it. The ladder's Rung 1 governs what happens **after a-r0 has been reported as failed**: the regimes R1–R7 may then be admitted and reported, but only as an explicitly regime-conditional statement carrying the words "in regime R_k", never as the primary claim, and always with a-r0's failed status reported first and in full. R7 (25 Mbit/s) is defined in `V025-CONTINGENCY-LADDER-AMENDMENT-1-R7-2026-09-08.md`; v1.6 §4's reference to R1–R7 means R1–R6 of the ladder plus that amendment.
5. **Margin provenance for the three contrasts.** δ = +0.5 % relative with its lower-bound rule was pre-registered for **each** of the three FULL − DROP contrasts in v1.2 §4 and contract v1 §D2; the ladder's Rung 0 restates it for the C3 (Level-B) contrast. This is a citation, not an expansion.
6. **Reporting rules carried from the review.** A regime that was not executed is reported as *skipped*, never as negative (R2: the provider fixes the user count at 100). Failure to clear +0.5 % means the benefit was **not established**, not that the effect is non-positive; the stronger statement requires a calibrated upper bound at or below zero. Every interval is printed with its contrast, endpoint, units, decision margin, method, sidedness, nominal level, whether coverage is marginal or simultaneous, the number of dates and learner seeds, the panel scope, and the measured calibration coverage with its Monte-Carlo uncertainty.
