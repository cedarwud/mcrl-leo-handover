## Two things recorded now because they matter later

### 1. The gain decays with training depth, and the decay is decelerating

| checkpoint | D0 mean EE | D3-T0 mean EE | D3-T0 gap (seed mean) | D2-T0 gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ep 100 | 99.50 M | 112.13 M | +12.71 % | +13.21 % |
| ep 200 | 103.13 M | 113.10 M | +9.72 % | +9.78 % |
| ep 300 | 103.78 M | 113.55 M | **+9.42 %** | +9.06 % |

The gap narrowed 3.0 pp between ep 100 and ep 200 but only **0.3 pp** between ep 200 and ep 300: `D0` spent the first
200 episodes closing an early deficit and then flattened (+0.65 M in the last 100), while `D3-T0` flattened too
(+0.45 M). Both arms are approaching plateaus separated by roughly 9.5 pp.

**Declared prospectively, before S1 runs:** S1's depth is already fixed at **1000 episodes × 3 seeds** (Amendment 6,
Amendment 4/5 regime), so no depth can be chosen from this result. All development evidence stops at ep 300, and S1
is 3.3 × deeper. The ep200→ep300 behaviour suggests a plateau near +9 %, but **nothing here proves the gap survives to
ep 1000**. A materially smaller gap at S1 is a legitimate outcome and is **not** evidence of a bug — it is the reason
S1 exists. This paragraph is written now so that neither "we always expected it" nor "something broke" is available
afterwards.

### 2. Imitating the teacher more closely does not help more

`t0_agreement` at ep 300 — the fraction of DEVVAL decisions matching T0:

| arm | k=3 | k=4 | k=5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| D0 | 0.369 | 0.362 | 0.364 |
| D3-T0 (hard margin) | 0.724 | 0.722 | 0.704 |
| D2-T0 (soft, τ = 0.3) | 0.752 | 0.752 | 0.741 |

The soft arm agrees with T0 **more** than the hard arm on all three seeds, yet has the slightly lower seed mean. So the
best policy found is not the closest imitation of T0: T0 is a useful prior that the learner improves on, not a target
to converge to. This is consistent with the catfish framing, and it also sharpens the question Amendment 12's second
null (`T0-XEP`) exists to answer — how much of the gain is T0's state-specific information versus the structural prior
its form carries. **Amendment 12 was written and committed (`2c8dfac6`) before this table was read.**
