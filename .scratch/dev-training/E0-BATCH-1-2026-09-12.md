## Functional reading (direction only; no statistical claim, no formal-evidence language)

1. **Does D0 learn at all?** Yes, directionally. Behaviour EE rises 5.49e07 -> 1.015e08 over the 100 episodes, served
   0.944 -> 0.995, the Q_E head's TD loss falls ~8x (0.195 -> 0.0253), and the greedy DEVVAL policy reaches 1.031e08 bit/J
   = 1.98x the RANDOM reference. It is still **below** both one-line rules (0.94 of MAX_NOMINAL_GAIN, 0.88 of T0) at 1/3 of
   the E0 ceiling, which is what "the kernel takes a real gradient and improves" looks like, not "the kernel wins".
2. **Does D2-T0 move toward T0, and does it beat D0?** Yes on both, directionally and consistently. Agreement with T0 on the
   greedy DEVVAL policy 0.380 -> 0.538 and T0-score regret 0.959 -> 0.405 (-58 %) against D0; DEVVAL EE +4.24 % with 23/24
   paired episodes ahead. It costs nothing in service (0.9982 vs 0.9984) and it **raises the rate floor**: p10 1.079e08 vs
   7.65e07 and min 4.94e06 vs 2.02e03 bit/s. Its own TD losses stay at D0's level (0.406 vs 0.390 on Q_B at ep 90-99), so
   alpha = 1 is not swamping the TD objective.
3. **Does the gain need T0's information?** Yes, directionally -- this is the point of the matched null. D2-null carries the
   identical loss shape, weight, temperature, schedule, mask and gradient path, differing only in which legal action carries
   which score, and it lands **below** D0 (-2.04 %, 18/24 paired episodes behind), with agreement 0.376 (D0's 0.380, i.e. no
   movement toward T0) and regret slightly worse than D0's. So the D2 effect at 100 episodes is not "an extra loss term" or
   "extra gradient magnitude": it is the teacher's action information.
4. **Teacher-loss level is not the signal.** The CE barely moves (3.229 -> 3.150 for D2-T0; 3.273 -> 3.263 for the null) while
   the agreement moves a lot. Inferred, not measured: at tau = 3 the target softmax(T0 score / 3) over ~26 legal actions is
   nearly uniform, so most of the CE is the target's own entropy (~ln 26 = 3.26, where the unlearnable null sits) and only
   ~0.11 nats of it is learnable. tau = 3 was selected on VAL for a clone whose 28 logits were free; here the same target has
   to be matched by a scalar score whose scale is pinned by the TD objective. That is a temperature question for E0b, not a
   defect.
