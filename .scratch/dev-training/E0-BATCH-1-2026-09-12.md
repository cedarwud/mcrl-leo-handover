## What this batch can and cannot decide
It can show whether the kernel learns at all under a real gradient (D0), whether the teacher term moves the deployed score
toward T0 (D2-T0 vs D0), and whether that movement needs T0's action information (D2-T0 vs D2-null, the matched null).
It cannot show that any arm beats any other: one seed, 100 episodes, no confidence statement, no formal episode set.
