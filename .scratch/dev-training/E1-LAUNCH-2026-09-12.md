## The reading rule, fixed before any E1 number exists
Only **ep 300 decides**; the 100 and 200 readouts exist to catch divergence, a bug or a catastrophic QoS failure, and no
configuration may change because an intermediate reading looks good. At ep 300, development reading, no formal claim and no
confidence interval:
- **Direction**: D3-T0 beats D0 on at least **2 of the 3 fresh seeds**, with the seed mean in the same direction.
- **QoS floors** (the existing numbers): served not worse than the comparison arm by more than **-0.5 pp**;
  `p10 >= 0.5 x` the comparison arm's; `bits ratio >= 0.95`.
- D2-T0 tau = 0.3 is read the same way, as the strongest soft comparator; no retuning on a small D2-versus-D3 difference.
- The design reopens only if D3-T0 fails that direction or the QoS floors, or if D2 shows a very consistent substantive
  advantage on all three fresh seeds -- and then the next step is a controller review, not inferring hyperparameters.
