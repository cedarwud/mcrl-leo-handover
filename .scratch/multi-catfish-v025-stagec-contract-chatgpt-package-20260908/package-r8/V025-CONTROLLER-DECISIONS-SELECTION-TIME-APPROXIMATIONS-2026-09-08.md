# V025 — controller decisions: selection-time approximations of a deployable coordinator (2026-09-08 ≈ 19:25 UTC; before any formal successor outcome)

Trigger: stage-4 real-world measurements on the quarantined world — 2 800 legal candidates, 2 912 catalogue rows per anchor, 0.0335 s per row for the exact 48-boundary immediate score, C2 forecasts unbatched (> 450 s per anchor), provider one-step construction 9.9–12.5 s. A complete anchor is far beyond the coordinator's own decision budget, so the exact-everything selector is not a deployable object either. The physics, labels and endpoint stay exact at 48 boundaries; **selection-time scoring** gets the approximations a real controller would use. All values below are sealed before any outcome; none may be changed after.

1. **Catalogue shortlist (replaces the "all legal options" unilateral set):** per user, the top-8 legal options by nominal single-link decoding margin at the decision instant (deployable shortlist) → ≤ 800 unilateral rows; pairwise among the top-10 users by exact nominal unilateral surplus × their top-2 options (≤ 180); per-active-beam evacuations (≈ 40); S0 top-two + evacuations (≤ 200). Catalogue ≈ 1 200 rows. The stage-3 item-10 diagnostic reports, per anchor, how many legal options fall outside the shortlist and the best excluded option's margin.
2. **Coarse selection grid:** selection-time immediate scores use 5 boundaries k ∈ {0, 12, 24, 36, 47} with trapezoid weights over the 30.08 s step; the committed profile, every label (C1/C2/C3) and the endpoint use all 48 boundaries. Report (no gate): top-1 agreement between the 5-boundary and 48-boundary scores on the synthetic and rehearsal anchors.
3. **Two-stage scoring:** stage 1 = immediate score C1 + Ψ (interaction) for all catalogue rows on the coarse grid; stage 2 = C2 continuation (three offsets, batched) only for the top-M = 64 rows by stage-1 score plus BASE, the incumbents and every S0 proposal; the selected profile is the argmax of the complete score within that set. M is sealed.
4. **Batched forecasts:** the three-offset continuation for the stage-2 set is one shared batch call per anchor (configuration × offset × boundary).
5. **Provider amortisation:** the world tape (33 steps × 48 boundaries) is constructed once per world and reused by all arms and settings; construction time is reported per world (≈ 7 min at the measured rate is acceptable); the per-anchor provider target of 1 s is withdrawn in favour of "once per world".
6. **Budget arithmetic (to be confirmed by the rehearsal):** ≈ 1 200 rows × (5/48) × 0.0335 s ≈ 4.2 s + 64 × 3 offsets ≈ 6 s + selection ≈ 11 s per anchor; a-r0 = 4 worlds × 90 anchors × 13 arms sharing the catalogue ≈ 1–2 core-hours; stride k = 1 expected; the sealed thinning order applies only if the rehearsal disagrees.
7. **Deadline consistency:** the 10 s compute budget (contract v1 §F2) is evaluated against the measured per-anchor time on the declared worker count; a miss executes the validated BASE.
8. **KATs:** shortlist superset report field; 5- vs 48-boundary agreement report; two-stage argmax equals the full-catalogue argmax on synthetic anchors where the true optimum is in the top-M (and the miss is reported when it is not); batched forecasts equal per-row forecasts to 1e-9.
