# Controller record — two independent strategic reviews reached the same verdict, and it is not the one I was working toward
Recorded 2026-09-10. Two reviews were commissioned with fresh context on different model families, given the same prompt, and explicitly told to question the frame rather than the details. They did not see each other's work.

## The verdict, in their own words
* One: "**The mechanism is the paper; the learned coordinator is an unproven follow-on project and should stop determining this paper's scope.**"
* The other: "**The mechanism is the contribution. The learned coordinator is not, and on the evidence already in hand it is not going to become one on this timeline.**"

Both are scoped to **this paper and this timeline**. Neither says the three-component goal is wrong or unreachable.

## The sentence that indicts my day
> The controller has treated today's discoveries as reasons to **repair** the original research programme. They are stronger reasons to **replace its primary question**.

That is accurate. I read every finding as "what to fix". Collectively they say "what to ask instead".

## What the reframed paper contains, and five of six parts are already measured
1. **Mechanism.** Under a rate target with equal-airtime multiplexing and adaptive coding, raising beam occupancy enlarges back-off room so a lower mode clears its threshold. Adding load to a beam can make it servable. Four constructive witnesses through the production scoring path, plus a reuse-colour control that drives the interaction to −5.9e−6 bits and isolates interference as necessary in one of them.
2. **Prevalence.** Qualifying coalitions at 30 of 30 real anchors, median energy-efficiency gain 9.118 %, range 4.156 to 22.756 %. Occupancy activation 28/30, complete beam evacuation 29/30, multi-aggressor relief **0/30**. About 61 % of active beam-anchor incidences sit at occupancy 1 or 2, the regime that delivers nothing.
3. **Ceiling.** A perfect-knowledge bounded set selector beats an exhaustively certified unilateral optimum by **+6.359 %** pooled energy efficiency over 20 anchors with served counts **up**, 1,957 against 1,939 against 826, robust across 18 circuit-accounting combinations at +4.84 % to +6.59 % and **larger at zero per-chain power**, the architecture an outside hardware review called fatal.
4. **Structure.** The interaction is a threshold on summed occupancy and the pairwise decomposition is measurably misspecified, with the third-and-above remainder exceeding 1e−3 on 38.50 % of 5,003 coalitions.
5. **Unreachability, which is what makes the paper matter.** The certified unilateral comparator needs up to 1,213 s against a 10 s budget, 91.55 % of it in irreducible coupled-power solves; iteration counts are bimodal at {0, 46, 50, 80, 86, 86} with nothing between 1 and 45, so no realistic budget increase converts an anchor; and the engine admits **no valid pruning certificate** because the hard decode thresholds defeat any Lipschitz enclosure. **Real headroom exists that exhaustive search cannot reach in budget.** That converts the motivation for approximate coordination from an assumption into a measurement.
6. **A negative result reported as one.** The learned interaction head does not beat a constant-zero predictor on realised efficiency, with item 4 as the diagnosis.

## The weakest point, named precisely: N
Every number above comes from one to four worlds, two dates, one constellation, 20 to 30 anchors. **There is no interval on the +6.36 %.** A referee asks for the distribution over geometries and there is nothing.

It is also the cheapest item on the board. The prior ceiling run took 694.6 s for 20 anchors on four processes, and the diagnostic is **learner-free**, so the contested train-versus-evaluation date-boundary question does not apply to it at all. Thirty dates at 20 anchors is single-digit core-hours. That measurement is now running.

I had been treating the date shortage as one large blocker. It is two separate problems and only the learner-side one is hard.

## Two frames I had not considered
**Nobody has measured the one-line policy.** The mechanism is a property of a **beam**, not of a coalition: no beam below occupancy three, consolidate or empty. What fraction of the +6.36 % that captures has never been measured, and both answers are valuable. If it captures most, a sub-millisecond physically motivated rule beats an exhaustive optimiser and the learner question retires. If it captures little, the coordination structure is demonstrably non-trivial, which the project currently asserts rather than shows. Now running.

**The objective may be the wrong one, and 0/30 is the tell.** With quality of service as a guard rather than an outcome, the optimiser has no reason to spread load ever. Reporting an efficiency-against-worst-decile-rate frontier would be a stronger claim, and the served counts rising rather than falling suggest **we may already have the stronger version and be reporting the weaker one**.
