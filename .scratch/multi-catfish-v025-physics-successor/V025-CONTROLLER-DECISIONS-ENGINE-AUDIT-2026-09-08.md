2. **B2 — factor arms cannot measure component marginals (design CHANGE).** The set decoder's score for FULL/DROP arms is the **sum of the declared targets**, not the exact realised objective: S(config) = C1(config) + C2(config) + C3(config), each the declared target in bits computed by the oracle from the same nominal physics (C1 whole-network difference surplus + Φ; C2 three-offset forecasts with −κ; C3 = interaction share per decision item 5 / Shapley for sets). The exact-F oracle is a **ceiling arm** (U1/J1/union certificates), never a factor arm. Marginals are measured on the realised pooled endpoint ΣB/ΣE, never on nominal scores. KATs: on a synthetic world with a constructed non-zero interaction, FULL and DROP_C3 select different configurations and the realised marginal equals the hand-computed value; with zero interaction they coincide; DROP_C1/DROP_C2 differ from FULL on fixtures where the dropped term is decisive.
