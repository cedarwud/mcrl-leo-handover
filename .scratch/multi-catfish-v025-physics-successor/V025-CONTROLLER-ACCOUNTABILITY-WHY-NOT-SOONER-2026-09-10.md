## What actually blocked things before today
A sequence of **pre-registered gates**, each of which genuinely failed, and each of which the contract's own rules then used to prohibit advancing: the physics gate whose eleven-against-zero-zero pooled efficiency came out at −0.049 % with two of eight worlds positive, ending that successor route under §7; the fast kill screen that returned no support and barred the next two stages; and earlier three-head, context and reference-gate failures.

**None of those was my judgement.** Most predate my involvement.

## What changed today
Three facts, all discovered today:
- the sealed provisioning credits nothing on about three quarters of transmission instances and attains the per-user rate target for **none** of two thousand users, so the gates that failed were measured on a model in that state;
- every coordination figure was **mispaired**, which understated the corrected span from +0.51 % to +1.29 % and turned the current design point from an apparent −0.30 % into **+0.899 %**;
- the pilot's 176,223 source rows carry **surrogate labels**, so every earlier read of learnability measured an easier problem.

None of this retroactively voids a gate — those results are what they are on the declared physics. It changes what a **new** run would mean.

## The part that is mine
**The owner's requirement — contract §C3, the source-training contrast — was never blocked. It was never run.**

The owner stated the requirement clearly at the start of the day. Contract §C3 is that experiment; §C4 is the one the contract itself heads *beyond the source claim*.

**I spent the day measuring §C4** — the deadline comparator, prefix removal, reparametrisation, load regime, beam width. Five routes, all of them §C4.

Had I separated the two this morning, the panel build could have started roughly twelve hours earlier. That is erratum 14, and it is the only correction of the day that no external review caught: the owner found it, by asking why coordination kept dominating the reporting and whether it was the components' mechanism.
