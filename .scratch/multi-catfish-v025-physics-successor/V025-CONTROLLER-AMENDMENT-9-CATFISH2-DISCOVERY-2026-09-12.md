- **D3 is an injection mechanism, not a catfish.**
- **T0 is a candidate source with very strong transfer evidence.** Its causal marginal under D3 is now closed on three
  development seeds (D3-T0 beats the paired D0 by +9.11 / +13.34 / +9.23 %, 24/24 each; the matched null is −39 to −55 %), so

  drop-one causal test. Being a renamed score term is not enough.

## 3. Stage 0 and its prospective reading rule

The full brief, with the candidate definitions, the measurement list and **the reading rule fixed before any counted
evaluation**, is `.scratch/catfish2-discovery/BRIEF.md`. In summary: two prospectively specified new candidates — **C-Q**, a
QoS / rate-tail specialist (T0's score restricted to candidates clearing an existing project rate threshold, using the user's own
observation-level predicted share), and **C-RC**, rate-qualified consolidation (prefer an already-lit beam only among candidates
that clear that threshold) — plus the legacy policies as controls, plus a diagnostic decomposition of T0 into its link endpoint
and its activation endpoint. A candidate advances only if it is deployable (or clones at `R_repr ≥ 0.5`), is non-dominated in
(EE, served, p10) or clearly complementary (`p10 ≥ 1.10 × T0`, served ≥ 0.995, EE ≥ 0.95 × T0), disagrees with T0 on ≥ 25 % of
shared states, shows positive value-weighted complementarity (candidate-better fraction ≥ 0.30 and `CR = Σ max(Δ,0) /
Σ |min(Δ,0)| ≥ 0.5` on the unilateral `Δ(bits − η₀·joules)`), and shows no QoS collapse. Survivors rank by `CR`, ties within 10 %
break on p10, **at most two advance, and zero is a legitimate answer**. The T0 decomposition counts as supported only if each
endpoint separately clears the same five conditions against the fused T0; otherwise T0 stays one candidate source and the
decomposition is rejected. No salami-slicing.

Each survivor gets one 100-episode DEV k = 0 canary through the already-working D3 injection, with a matched random-legal null on
its own DEV-NULL identity, read against D0 at the same depth; k = 1 only for a k = 0 survivor; nothing runs to 300. Multi-teacher
integration waits until two sources independently survive, and its arbitration rule between conflicting teacher actions is
declared **before** the first `T0 + C2` run, grounded in each teacher's own advantage or a state-dependent gate rather than an
arbitrary weight. The minimum multi-catfish test is FULL versus DROP_T0 versus DROP_C2 versus the matched control: **the count is
earned by positive drop-one causal marginal**, not by the number of named heuristics.
