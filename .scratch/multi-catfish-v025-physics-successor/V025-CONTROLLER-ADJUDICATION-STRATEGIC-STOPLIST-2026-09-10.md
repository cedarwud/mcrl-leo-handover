# Controller adjudication — what I accept from the strategic review, what I do not, and what only the owner can settle
Recorded 2026-09-10 against the two independent strategic reviews of 2026-09-09/10. Neither review is binding; both were commissioned by me and neither was given the owner's two hard requirements as a constraint. That omission matters for exactly one item below.

## The six stops, ruled one by one

**Stop the six-arm, many-seed success panel.** *Accept.* Six arms and many learner seeds ahead of one useful learned decision procedure is expensive rehearsal of an unanswered question. Nothing is being run on that path today.

**Stop growing the corpus toward 51k–93k labels on the strength of an extrapolated curve.** *Accept, with the reason restated.* The decisive observation is not the extrapolation, it is that at the largest measured point the head still loses on both regret and realised efficiency — 10.3098 against 10.3178 Mbit/J — while its exact-best frequency improves. More accurate values have not yet produced a better rule. Growth resumes only if a measurement shows accuracy converting into decisions.

**Stop treating a larger timeout or faster certification as the fix for the unilateral comparator.** *Accept.* The profiling shows genuinely late winners, 41 to 86 improving iterations and a longest uncensored run of 1,213 s. The tail is real work, not proof overhead. The two-arm split — offline certification for diagnosis, a separately declared anytime comparator for evaluation — is already required by contract v1.2 item 6, so this is not a new gate.

**Stop sweeping the disputed circuit constant as a rescue.** *Accept.* The fixed-assignment sensitivity already answered whether that constant creates the sign; it does not, and the effect is larger at zero watts. More points do not address the real objection.

**Stop broad defect adjudication as the default activity.** *Accept in principle, and I am the one who has been violating it.* Repair only what the selected result needs.

**Stop rebuilding the first two heads merely to preserve the three-component story.** *Reject, on the owner's standing requirement.* The review is right that the mechanism study does not need them, and right that repairing two surrogates to protect a narrative is bad science. But the owner's requirement is not narrative: each of the three components must genuinely raise pooled efficiency, and publishing a negative for one of them is explicitly not acceptable. The sufficiency diagnostic has since shown that on the current encoders the first head has held-out explained variance of −0.336 — worse than predicting the mean — with a proved collision that no deterministic predictor can escape. Under that defect a three-head run cannot be a fair test of the first two, and a null from it would be uninterpretable. So the encoder repair is a **precondition for answering the owner's question at all**, not a rescue of a story. It stays. The review did not have this requirement in front of it.

## Where the review anticipated today's result
Its closing section asked whether the occupancy blackout should exist at all, and proposed comparing a service-aware power policy that can allocate enough power for a feasible mode without waiting for two more users to arrive. The ladder job, dispatched before I read that section, is running precisely that comparison, and its interim numbers already show zero no-mode events under margin provisioning against 55.7 % under the sealed law. The review called this "a new scientific intervention, not a permissible silent change to the sealed benchmark", and it is being run that way: on a copy, with the declared physics untouched.

If the coordination gain largely survives the change, the mechanism claim strengthens and gains a much better story — a genuine convexity between superlinear power growth under consolidation and per-chain circuit cost under spreading. If it largely vanishes, the contribution becomes a diagnosis of the control law and its remedy, which the review judges potentially more useful than teaching three networks to compensate for it. Either way the ladder job decides it, and I will not pre-empt it.

## Three cautions I am adopting as constraints on how anything gets written
- The bounded-search advantage is **not** a mathematical ceiling. Its comparator is one specified unilateral procedure's local fixed point, not the best obtainable by every unilateral strategy and not a certified global optimum. The claim is that coordinated moves improve those certified local endpoints.
- "Served" in the current instrumentation means positive decoding time. It is **not** rate-target attainment and **not** uninterrupted service, so the 1,939→1,957 improvement does not by itself discharge the contracted service conditions.
- No number of anchors or seeds converts one completed ephemeris date into independent date replication.

## Owner decisions this does not settle
The reviews split on the evaluation date pool and one of them proposed three further options; the comparator positioning, the beamwidth convention and the strategic reframing itself all remain the owner's. Recorded here only so that accepting five of six stops is not mistaken for accepting the reframing.
