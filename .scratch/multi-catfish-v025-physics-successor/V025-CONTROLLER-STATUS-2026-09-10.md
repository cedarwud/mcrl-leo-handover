## The owner's requirement, stated exactly

This is **contract §C3**, the neutral-source substitution contrast: for a named route, its training source is replaced by a neutral one while all heads are retained, updated and deployed. It is **not** §C4, which the contract itself heads *coordination attribution, beyond the source claim*. I conflated the two all day; that is erratum 14.

**§C3 has never been measured at scale.** The only run touching it was a two-seed, one-world, five-anchor pilot in which six of ten decisions fell outside the training support.
