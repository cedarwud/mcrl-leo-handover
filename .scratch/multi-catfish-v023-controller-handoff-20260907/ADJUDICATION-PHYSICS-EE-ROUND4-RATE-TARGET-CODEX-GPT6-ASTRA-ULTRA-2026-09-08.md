- **Finite-demand option:** \(B_u=\min(d_u,\int R_u\,dt)\). C1 retains energy savings and delivery-shortfall reductions, C2 forecasts future power/availability, and C3 trades consolidation savings against congestion and cap failures. Demand clipping alone creates no energy mechanism; rate inversion or explicit duty control must supply it. [Demand/L1 distinction](/home/sat/mcrl-v023-codex-astra/trackb-next-lever-final.md:17)
