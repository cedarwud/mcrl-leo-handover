## Addendum (02:25 UTC) — the catalogue-anchor comparison, which is the point of this pilot
The development evidence says joint moves around the **carrier** anchor are strongly sub-additive (users collide), and that an exhaustive unilateral best response captures most of the available gain. Mathematically, re-anchored at a certified unilateral local optimum u every single-user move satisfies dᵢ^u ≤ 0, so an improving joint move from u must have a **positive** interaction residual. A coordinator anchored at the carrier is therefore looking where interactions are negative; a coordinator anchored at u is looking exactly where joint escapes must be positive if they exist at all.
Run the pilot with **both catalogue anchors** and report them side by side:
- **P-a⁰** (as sealed): catalogue built around the carrier proposal a⁰.
- **P-u** (pilot-only variant): first compute the iterated exact unilateral optimum u (same evaluator, guards and objective), then build the joint part of the catalogue around u — all pairwise moves among the top-10 users by |dᵢ^u|, per-beam evacuations from u, and the S0 proposals; the unilateral part is unchanged.
For each anchor report: the realised pooled EE of FULL(S3), S0 and S_UNI; the fraction of anchors with a strictly improving joint escape from u; the distribution of Ψ_A^u for the selected coalition; V_CA; and the extra wall time. **P-u is a pilot variant only** — it does not change the sealed catalogue and its numbers are `PILOT_NOT_CLAIM`; its purpose is to tell the controller, in hours, whether a positive interaction term exists at all in this physics. If P-u shows a non-trivial fraction of positive Ψ_A^u, that is the design the controller will consider amending into the sealed plan before the matrix; if it shows essentially none, C3 will be reported as an evaluated layer and no further redesign is attempted.
