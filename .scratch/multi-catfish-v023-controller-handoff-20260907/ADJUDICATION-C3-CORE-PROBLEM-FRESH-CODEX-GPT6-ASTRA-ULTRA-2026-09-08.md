**A. Domain prior**

“Load balancing should affect EE” is **regime-conditional**. Neither balancing nor consolidation guarantees improvement. For matched totals, the exact condition is \(\Delta B-\eta_{\mathrm{BASE}}\Delta E>0\), with the required service constraints satisfied; balancing load is not itself this objective. [02:10](/home/sat/mcrl-v023-astra-c3core/02-GLOBAL-VIEWS.md:10)

- **Rate-target power control:** with bandwidth \(w_u\), channel gain \(h_u\), and target \(R_u^\star\), Shannon inversion gives \(p_u^{req}=(N_0w_u+I_u)(2^{R_u^\star/w_u}-1)/h_u\). Under full-band TDMA, replace the exponent by \(R_u^\star/(\tau_uW)\) and average PA consumption over airtime. Relieving congestion can therefore reduce required power; worse destination channels or additional active beams can outweigh that saving. The distinction between TDMA and FDMA is essential. [11:124](/home/sat/mcrl-v023-astra-c3core/11-ASTRA-PHYSICS-ROUND3.md:124)
- **Interference, activation and PA:** association changes co-channel interference and hence rates or required powers. Consolidation can switch off PA/circuit resources; spreading can improve interference or prevent rate-target infeasibility. PA efficiency determines the DC cost of either choice. Congestion matters to useful EE through delivered demand, queues, losses and QoS—not merely user counts. [11:15](/home/sat/mcrl-v023-astra-c3core/11-ASTRA-PHYSICS-ROUND3.md:15)
- **Before:** full buffer, uncapped Shannon, equal-airtime sharing, beam RF \(=\max_u p_u\), no rate target, and a permanently unsaturated, **concave** square-root PA supply curve. There was no general occupancy→required-power mechanism, but activation, power leadership, interference and heterogeneous rates still provided coordination levers. Thus “no energy lever whatsoever” is false. [power/rate implementation](/home/sat/mcrl-leo-handover-e1/src/mcrl/env/link_budget.py:439)
- **Consolidation is also conditional:** merging two identical singleton beams halves aggregate rate as well as approximately halving power. The earlier claims that spreading is monotonically bad and consolidation monotonically good overreach. [11:44](/home/sat/mcrl-v023-astra-c3core/11-ASTRA-PHYSICS-ROUND3.md:44)
- **Successor:** I follow the owner’s chosen memoryless angle-aware control with rate targets, keeping fixed RF as reference. However, file 11’s concrete alternative specifies **constant SINR** \(\gamma^\star=5.6600\), not a load-dependent per-user rate target. That distinction must be resolved: constant target SINR alone does not establish the missing mechanism. The supplied checkout still implements the old recurrence; successor results are not available here. [11:146](/home/sat/mcrl-v023-astra-c3core/11-ASTRA-PHYSICS-ROUND3.md:146), [recurrence](/home/sat/mcrl-leo-handover-e1/src/mcrl/env/link_budget.py:379)
