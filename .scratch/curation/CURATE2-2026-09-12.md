## Highest-risk rows, and why

Across all eight families, these are the rows most likely to be misquoted or mis-compared:

1. **E0-19 (+19.8 %).** The E0 development champion beats the frozen baseline MODQN by "+19.8 %" on DEVVAL. The
   project already has a famous "+19.8 %" — `CT-03` / `CS-06`, MNG vs trained on the **local unpinned** archive,
   now standing only as a negative control. **Same headline number, unrelated procedures, different host, archive,
   episode set, arms and estimand.** The row says so in its status cell; anyone quoting "+19.8 %" without the
   prefix is quoting an ambiguity.
2. **E0-02 (94,413,179 bit/J for checkpoint `e6b063ef…`).** A **fifth** value for the same frozen checkpoint now
   circulates: 93,110,907.97 and 93,137,893.02 (§1a, local unpinned, seeds 42/1337/7), 89,911,720 (EC-01, sat
   pinned, evaluation set), 93,902,816.57 (EC-02, sat pinned, calibration set) and 94,413,179 (E0-02, sat pinned,
   DEVVAL). None is comparable with another. Both EC-01 and E0-02 carry the warning in their status cells.
3. **OR-01 (A-real-floor +6.104 %).** The number is real and the arithmetic clears the +3.3 % line, yet the source
   itself says the gain **does not count** because the cell is throughput-degenerate (p10 0.282 ×, 32.2 % of
   served user-steps under the floor) — and it explicitly flags that the two readings "point opposite ways on the
   arithmetic alone" without resolving it. This is the single easiest row in §5 to quote as a positive result.
4. **TSQ-02 (calibration `R_repr` 0.0916 / 0.0508).** The **only** §5 rows that are in-sample by construction —
   the training corpus *is* the calibration set. The row says so; a `TSQ-` calibration figure quoted as
   out-of-sample would be wrong in kind, not degree.
5. **EC-04 / EC-05 (+41.36 % and +23.91 %).** Search winners, never rules' scores, and lower bounds on the
   *one-step local* optimum (240/240 base steps hit the sweep budget with η still rising). EC-05 is on **6**
   episodes and must be read only against EC-06's same-episode references — never against EC-04's 24-episode
   numbers. Both rows say so; the 6-vs-24 episode trap applies to EC-05, EC-07..EC-13 and B1C-02/03.
6. **LP-03 (LP-prev(2, 0), +7.561 %).** A grid-search winner over 24 cells scored on the set it is reported on
   (selection optimism, acknowledged by LP-26), and it **fails** the downstream bits-ratio ≥ 0.95 condition
   (0.887). The cell the downstream gates actually cite is **LP-05**, LP-prev(1, 0) at +6.662 %.
7. **B1C-02 (+24.614 %).** The largest positive percentage in the `B1C-` family, on a **FAIL** verdict, on **6**
   episodes, on the local host. Its own status cell records that the rejecting condition is the bits ratio (0.867),
   not p10.
8. **H4-16 / H4-17 (the ranking tables).** These mix `[A]` (measured by the probe) and `[P]` (quoted verbatim from
   the CF3 pilot) figures in one table. The `[P]` numbers carry the pilot's provenance block, not the probe's, and
   H4-17's four learner figures are **calibration-set** readings (§0 trap 6). Rows H4-16 and H4-17 must never be
   read across each other.

4. **`E0-BATCH-1-2026-09-12.md` contains a self-superseding adoption.** Line 309 adopts τ = 1 as the D2

   selects τ = 0.3. Anyone quoting line 309 alone quotes a withdrawn decision. Recorded as E0-15; the freeze
   document (`E0FR:25-26`) carries the surviving choice.

   sweep order" — and the T_SEQ screen's own reverse-order check (TSQ-06) can therefore only compare clone with
